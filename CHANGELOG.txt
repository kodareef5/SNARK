v0.0.14 — 2026-11-26

City structures
- A ruined storey gives way when most of its original supports are gone. On newly built maps, fallen window frames lie low enough to walk over, and hanging slabs leave room underneath.
- New city builds keep plants out of paved traffic lanes. Four sizes of billboard line the roads, from small street signs to highway displays.

Doors
- Hinged doors and Tek doors respect tribe access. A blocked door or a Tek field that stays open leaves nearby survivors in place.

Resources
- All 25 resource drops have their own models and matching inventory icons. Look for the shape of the charcoal, pelt or folded freight order on the ground and in your pack.

City maps
- The map screen requests terrain around the point you select. Roadside lights keep their spacing where a road leaves the map and comes back.

v0.0.13 — 2026-11-25

Las Vegas
- The Sphere opens the Strip show with Royal Flush. A horse race circles the ball, a mirrorball reflects the Shanghai skyline, and Jupiter’s clouds drift overhead. The 3-minute 24-second show casts its colors onto nearby streets.
- Turn off Video on screens in Interface to show still posters.

Building
- Snap triangle foundations, ramps, large walls, double doors and dinosaur gates into your base. Roof hips and ridges join across corners.
- Copy a base into a building plan your tribe can fill piece by piece. Destroyed pieces scatter fragments of their own material.
- Craft 22 Tek building pieces at the Fabricator. They fit Metal structures and have 25% more health. Set adjoining Tek wall panels to door mode to open a passage for your tribe.
- Build a Tek workstation at the Fabricator, then use it to craft plasma rifles and pistols. The structure menu shows station capacity and Tek wall mode.

Cities
- Miami, Tampa, San Francisco, Los Angeles and Las Vegas now stream detailed ground and buildings around you. Distant blocks keep their skyline as you cross the city.
- Operators can rebuild each city from its Maps v3 kit. Start a fresh world for a rebuilt city and keep the old save with its map.

Joining a world
- Use the title as soon as it appears. Play brings a returning survivor back to their world, and new survivors can choose starting areas from maps and photographs.
- The loading screen shows progress into the world. On slow connections or with Data Saver enabled, live previews start only when you ask for them.
- The first download is smaller. The browser builds city ruins from their shared shapes. It loads the building editor, inventory and crafting panels when you need them, and the media player when a prop screen comes into view.
- Fixed a Chrome cache stall that could leave downloaded world art unsaved. Returning to the same area can now reuse those files.

Blue Glass HUD
- Desktop players can open their map, inventory, and action menus from the dock beside the hotbar. On touchscreens, the same controls open from wheels at the edges.
- Expand the corner map in place. The radio keeps a separate draft for each chat channel.

Map events
- Fly the XF-51 Wraith to intercept the UFO. Its shield opens when its beam fires. Bring it down and recover loot from the wreck.

Crowded worlds
- Nearby animals stay visible when a crowd fills the view. A slow connection can catch up with the current world as soon as it drains.

Operator tools
- Open the cluster console for world maps, population and chat. Each world has its own tools, including a live map and survivor activity replay.
- Schedule map events or call one from the Events page. On the Permissions page, assign staff roles by survivor or tribe and review changes in the admin log.
- Publish the title and loading shots separately in Scene Studio. In Screens, preview and publish separate channels for the Sphere, roadside billboards, TVs, and laptops. Channels can mix still images and clips.

v0.0.12 — 2026-11-24

Street salvage and driving
- Strip a parked wreck with a tool to recover parts, fuel and metal. Other players see the fittings come off and the shell disappear as you work.
- The chase camera holds its distance when a Zephyr boosts through a turn.

Combat
- Craft a plasma pistol or a scoped automatic plasma rifle at the fabricator. Their bolts take time to reach a target and can be dodged.
- Rockets leave a visible smoke trail and burst on impact. Nearby players see the same blast, including players who join while a rocket is in flight.
- Fists alternate from a guard. Hatchets swing one-handed and picks swing with both hands. A landed hit on a tree or rock scatters splinters or grit.

Experience
- Survivors and nearby tames earn experience over time. Kills, harvesting and crafting share experience with nearby tribemates. Riders earn a share of their mount’s gains.
- Saddled raptors can harvest a corpse with a bite. The bar below the hotbar tracks your next level.

Wiki
- Browse creatures, equipment and cities from the wiki sidebar. Inspect models in 3D, play sounds on demand and see gear and landmarks photographed in the game.

v0.0.10 — 2026-11-22

Streets
- Fitted every street as a line and typed it by rules: highway, boulevard, downtown, main street, tidy and rough residential, alley and industrial.
- Road, verge and walk edges run straight at any angle.
- Kerbs, gutters and walks bend along the street and round every corner. They drop flush, with tactile pads, at crossings and drives.
- Downtown walks reach the building line. Street signs, lamps and crossings stand on the new walks.

Cities
- Raised the Sextant, the Louver Lantern and the Tide Eye in Tampa, San Francisco, Los Angeles and Las Vegas, on decks graded into the hills.
- Walls weather from a recipe made on the graphics card, so no bay of a building repeats its stains.

Play
- Added supply drops: capsules cast from the obelisks land across each city by tier, from a drop book an admin can edit.
- Added ambient people who walk and drive the streets, and panic when things turn violent.
- Added optics. The Meridian visor zooms to 1,000 times with night and heat views: hold N. The spyglass reads a range and marks a creature for the tribe. The fabricated sniper rifle has a 4 to 40 times scope with a zero and a held breath.
- Added the Zephyr, a third sports body, and cold-gas thrusters for cars: Shift boosts, Q hops and flips.
- New survivors start with a saddled Pteranodon in a soul ball.

Sound & loading
- Sound fades in and out. Nothing snaps on.
- The world's sounds load after the first frame, which now waits for less than it did in v0.0.9.

Tools
- Added bug report mode: /notes and F8 take a note with pictures, the place and a performance record. /perf saves a performance report.
- Added /budget, a live panel of the frame's time and what spends it.

Engine
- Keys, the HUD and the frame are lists a feature adds one line to. The server's tick is a list of systems.
- Tests run by area, with a smoke set that takes seconds.
