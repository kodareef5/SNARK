v0.0.10 — 2026-11-22

Streets
- Fitted every street as a line and typed it by rules: highway, boulevard, downtown, main street, tidy and rough residential, alley and industrial.
- Road, verge and walk edges run straight at any angle.
- Kerbs, gutters and walks bend along the street and round every corner. They drop flush, with tactile pads, at crossings and drives.
- Downtown walks reach the building line. Street signs, lamps and crossings stand on the new walks.

Cities
- Raised the Sextant, the Louver Lantern and the Tide Eye in Tampa, San Francisco, Los Angeles and Las Vegas, on decks graded into the hills.
- Walls weather from a recipe made on the graphics card, so no bay of a building repeats its stains.

Play
- Added supply drops: capsules cast from the obelisks land across each city by tier, from a drop book an admin can edit.
- Added ambient people who walk and drive the streets, and panic when things turn violent.
- Added optics. The Meridian visor zooms to 1,000 times with night and heat views: hold N. The spyglass reads a range and marks a creature for the tribe. The fabricated sniper rifle has a 4 to 40 times scope with a zero and a held breath.
- Added the Zephyr, a third sports body, and cold-gas thrusters for cars: Shift boosts, Q hops and flips.
- New survivors start with a saddled Pteranodon in a soul ball.

Sound & loading
- Sound fades in and out. Nothing snaps on.
- The world's sounds load after the first frame, which now waits for less than it did in v0.0.9.

Tools
- Added bug report mode: /notes and F8 take a note with pictures, the place and a performance record. /perf saves a performance report.
- Added /budget, a live panel of the frame's time and what spends it.

Engine
- Keys, the HUD and the frame are lists a feature adds one line to. The server's tick is a list of systems.
- Tests run by area, with a smoke set that takes seconds.
