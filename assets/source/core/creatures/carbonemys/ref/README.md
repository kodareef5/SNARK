# Carbonemys

[Native model, saddle and motion sheet](native-review.png) · [All six shell levels from four angles](shell-levels.png)

The living animal spans 2.84 × 1.89 × 4.70 m, including the feet, head and tail. The original 3.40 m carapace has five vertebral, eight costal and 24 marginal shields. The plastron has its own ventral scute field. A three-joint neck folds to the side in guard and torpor. On land the feet bear weight under the shell. In water the webbed forefeet sweep out and back.

## Reference and reconstruction

- Cadena et al. describe the Carbonemys cofrinii skull UF/IGM 41 and illustrate dorsal, ventral and lateral views. Its condylobasal length is 16 cm, with maximum indicated length 21 cm and width 13 cm. The wide jaw surface and skull outline inform the playable head. Their 173 cm carapace UF/IGM 71 is a separate find from the same horizon, designated Pelomedusoides shell taxon A. Its association with the skull is uncertain. [Primary specimen paper, figures 2–4 and table 1](https://stri-apps.si.edu/docs/publications/pdfs/STRI-W_Jaramillo_2012_Cadena_carbonemys_cerrejon.pdf)
- The original Wildcard dossier informs the game's domed, ridged silhouette, substantial limbs and riding scale. The exact bosses, colored growth bands, soft tissue, neck motion and saddle construction are original interpretations for this asset. [Studio Wildcard Carbonemys dossier](https://survivetheark.com/index.php?/gallery/image/125-carbonemys/)
- Innerscene identifies its Hermann’s tortoise study as CC0. The public preview was inspected for the relationship of the marginal band to the recessed limbs. This terrestrial species offers a comparative reference. All native models, UVs, maps and motions here were generated by the authoring scripts in this folder. [Creator’s model and CC0 declaration](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/hermann-s-tortoise-88ac8979)

## Native checks

The body and rig use `core:carbonemys`. The worn mesh `core:saddle_carbonemys/worn` shares the shell bone in the same pack. The shell, plastron and bridges use torso zone 2, exposed neck and head use head zone 1, the four limbs use their respective arm, hand, leg and foot zones, and the tail uses zone 11.

The walk covers 1.26 m in 36 ticks, the run 1.26 m in 18 ticks and the distinct swim 3.2 m in 30 ticks. During the 25-sample bite, the mouth socket on tick 12 reaches 2.577 m forward and 0.659 m above the animal’s origin. The saddle seat at rest is 2.036 m high and 0.730 m forward.

To reproduce from the repository root:

```sh
bun tools/art.ts core:carbonemys --no-sheet
bun assets/source/core/creatures/carbonemys/review.ts var/carbonemys/views-delivery
bun assets/source/core/creatures/carbonemys/shell-review.ts var/carbonemys/shell-review
```

The sheets use the repository's CPU rasterizer and packed geometry. The principal sheet uses the source RGBA material mips. The levels sheet holds the camera distance constant and enlarges the flat fallback levels for comparison. These views do not exercise device texture compression. The neck and unarmored head retain a rounded soft-tissue profile. The last levels simplify individual toes and scute peaks into the silhouette.

The native shell coverage check samples 6,384 points on an interior ellipsoid from 40 directions at each level. Across 1,532,160 projections, every sample had a drawn pixel in front of it. The terminal body has 320 triangles and enters at about five pixels tall on Lowpoly. The flat LOD1 front-quarter capture previously contained 3,012 pale near-neutral pixels on the animal. Giving the dyed base an earthen value reduced this count to zero at the same 420 × 310 framing. The six dye masks retain their original weights.

The packed male and female survivor bodies were sampled in the engine's default riding pose at the final seat. The closest hand and foot vertices to their controls were 12–18 mm away. At 60% of the run clip's nominal speed, the existing gait test measured 8.5 mm of planted-foot spread for the adult and 4.3 mm at half size, down from 65.1 and 32.5 mm. All 12 walk/run speed and size combinations met the existing tolerance.
