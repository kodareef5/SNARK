# Dodo: trait sheet

The few traits that make players recognise ARK's dodo, written for the designer (D1), the modeller (D2) and the texture maker (D4). It describes ARK's dodo as players know it, not the real extinct bird.

**Evidence.** Official pictures: the wiki's in-game renders of both games' models (one per colour region), in-game screenshots on the wiki, the official dossier painting, the creature icon, the model sheet for the official animated series, a pose screenshot from the official forums, and ASA photo-mode shots in the official Community Crunch posts. Fan art: 32 pieces by 32 artists, from realistic paintings to pixel art, felt and clay. Every source is in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

**Ratios are by eye.** They come from side views in those pictures, not from measurement. Treat every number as "about", and use the ranges.

**ASE and ASA.** ARK: Survival Evolved's 2015 model is the dodo most players picture, and it is the base here. ARK: Survival Ascended's 2023 model keeps the same silhouette with a bigger beak, a shaggier hood and much finer feathers. Notes say where they differ.

## What matters, most important first

1. **The beak.** Oversized, deep and heavy, with a rounded tip that hooks down past the lower jaw. It dominates the head in every one of the 32 fan pieces and in every official picture. Many versions make the tip dark and the base pale (the dossier painting; 20 of 32 fan pieces). In the game the whole beak is one colour region, often a different colour from the face.
2. **The bare face under a feathered hood.** Bare, wrinkled skin runs from the base of the beak back to just behind the eye. The crown and nape stay feathered, so the face looks set into a hood with a ragged edge (27 of 32 fan pieces; both game models). The hood is its own colour region and in game often reads as a cap of a second colour. The face is usually pale and warm: yellow, tan or cream.
3. **The silhouette.** A fat pear or egg of a body, widest at the belly and rump, chest raised. A thick neck rises from the front of the body in an S and holds the big head up above the back. Short, stout legs. Stubby wings folded on the flanks. A tuft of tail feathers rising from the top of the rump.
4. **The dim look.** A small eye set high at the back edge of the bare patch, under a heavy brow. It stares blankly, sleepily or grumpily (27 of 32 fan pieces). Nothing about the head looks alert or clever. The official icon draws it heavy-lidded and grumpy.
5. **Scale-like feathers.** ARK's dodo is covered in rounded feathers overlapping in regular rows, so the body reads like scales or a pinecone. Both game models do this, and so do nearly all realistic fan pieces. Cartoons drop it, so it carries "ARK" more than "dodo".
6. **Small ARK touches.** Weaker but specific to ARK: one or two thin plumes with a spot or leaf tip rising from the nape or back (the dossier painting, the ASE model, 7 of 32 fan pieces); a pale or lavender tail plume (the dossier; 4 fan pieces); a contrasting patch or tips on the wing (the game's sixth colour region; 9 fan pieces).

The first four say "dodo". The hood, the brow, the scale-like feathers and the colour variety say "ARK's dodo". The plumes and the lavender tail are the most ARK-specific details of all, which makes them the details most likely to read as copied.

## Silhouette and stance

- **Side view.** An egg tilted up at the front. The neck leaves the top front of the body and curves up and slightly back, then forward into the head, so the head sits above the chest. The beak points forward and a little down. The tail tuft lifts above the line of the back at the rear. The legs come out from under the middle of the belly; the thighs are hidden in feathers, so only the scaly lower legs and feet show.
- **Front view.** Narrower than the side view suggests: a pear with the wide end down. The head and beak sit on top on the thick neck. The wings show as small flaps on each side. The feet stand under the body, a little apart.
- **Back view.** A round rump with the tail tuft on top, the hood on the back of the head, and the wing tips at the sides.
- **Stance.** Upright and front-heavy, weight over the feet. The body tilts about 15 to 30 degrees chest-up in game, and up to 45 in fan art. Standing still it looks slightly slumped.
- **Startled pose.** The other pose that turns up in official pictures (a pose named "Startled" on the official forums, and one wiki screenshot): beak wide open, head thrown up, both wings flung out from the sides.

## Colouring and where colour sits

The game has six colour regions. Their layout is what tells you where colour sits.

| Region | Where | Typical in game and official art |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Body and neck feathers, inner wing | One main colour: browns, greys, greens, reds, blues; ASA mostly browns, greys and creams. The dossier is brown with darker mottling; the animated series is blue-grey. |
| Face | Bare skin from beak base around the eye | Pale and warm: tan, yellow, cream, light brown. |
| Beak | The whole beak | Dark grey, black, green, dark red or brown. The dossier painting has a dark tip on a yellow base; the animated series makes it orange. |
| Forehead, Neck and Feet | Brow strip over the eye, throat patch, legs and toes | Grey, yellow, green or brown; scaly. |
| Head | The feathered hood on crown and nape | Often a second colour, a cap against the body. |
| Wings and Patterning | Flight feathers of the wing, faint pattern over the back | A pale or contrasting patch on the folded wing, pale tips. |

- **The contrast lives on the head.** Face, beak and hood are three regions within the head, so the head is the most colourful part even when the body is plain. In 27 of 32 fan pieces the face colour differs from the body.
- **The wing patch is the second accent.** In game it often shows as a pale blotch on the flank.
- **No strong belly shading.** The game models show none; the animated series uses a pale belly; only 2 or 3 fan pieces do. It is optional.
- **Body colour carries nothing.** Players dye creatures, and no colour is in more than 8 of 32 fan pieces.

## How it reads near, far and tiny

- **Close (a few metres).** The beak and its hook, the wrinkled face, the small eye under the brow, the hood's ragged edge, the feather rows, the wing patch, the leg scales and claws.
- **Middle (about 10 to 30 m).** A round dark body, a thick upright neck and a big head with a bright beak and face. The wing patch reads as a blotch. The legs almost vanish. In the wiki's beach screenshot, the coloured heads are what you notice first.
- **Far, or a few pixels.** A ball on a short stalk with a hook on top: the body, the neck rising at the front, and the head bulging forward. Like a bowling pin with a beak. The tail tuft is a bump at the back. Wings and legs disappear.
- **The lowest level of detail** must keep the round body, the neck at the front, the oversized forward head and beak, and the stubby stance. A few dozen triangles should do it if those masses are right.
- **The official creature icon** is the head alone in profile: the bulbous hooked beak, the heavy-lidded eye under the brow, the ragged hood and the thick neck. The game's own designers chose the head to stand for the whole creature.

## The quality bar in official pictures

- **ASE (2015 model).** The feathers are rounded scallops in regular rows, painted with relief, so the body reads as scales up close. The rows stay visible to middle distance. The outline is broken by feather cards: the edges of the body, wing and tail look ragged and soft, not smooth. The face is bare skin with deep folds around the eye and the gape. The beak has a pitted, matte surface. The legs show distinct scales and long claws. Realistic surfaces on a slightly cartoon shape.
- **ASA (2023 model).** Individual feathers in overlapping layers, each with a fine fringe and a bright edge, a dense ruff around the face, fine pores and creases in the skin, a modelled mouth with a tongue, and separate flight feathers on the wing. Clearly a higher bar than ASE.
- **Far away, in both.** The feather rows average out into the body colour, and only the head colours and the wing patch remain as shapes.

The ASE level is the comparable target for our feather material and maps: rows that read up close and at middle distance, a ragged soft outline, wrinkled skin and scaled legs. The ASA level is above it.

## What does not matter

- Body colour, eye colour, eye size, leg colour.
- Whether the wings are folded or raised in a given pose.
- The tail's colour, and whether it is a plume, a fan or a few feathers.
- Neck length anywhere within the range above.
- Sex: no visible difference was found between males and females.
- Accuracy to the real bird. ARK's dodo is its own caricature.
- Exact feather counts, the number of plumes, the exact toe shape beyond three forward and one back.

## Where ours can differ

Options for the designer, not decisions. None of these would cost recognition, as long as the ranked traits above stay.

- **Markings.** A pattern on the body (bars, speckles, mottling, scalloped edges in a second tone), a different shape or place for the wing patch, a pale belly, a different split between hood and body.
- **Head and beak detail.** The beak's exact shape within "big, deep, hooked": a sharper parrot hook, a rounder knob, a ridge, or a band in place of a dark tip. Where the nostril sits, the line of the gape, the texture of the bare face (smooth, wrinkled, warty), the brow's shape, the hood's shape (tight cap, shaggy mane, a short crest). Dropping or replacing the spot-tipped plumes is the clearest single way to look like our own.
- **Feather treatment.** Softer, fluffier contour feathers in place of the scale-like rows, longer shaggy feathers at the flanks, feather tips edged in a second colour, or a different size and rhythm of the rows. The scale-like rows say "ARK"; changing them moves us away from ARK fastest, so change them by degree.
- **Tail and wings.** A tail fan or a curl in place of a plume, in a colour other than lavender. Wing stubs with a few long primaries, or rounder ones.
- **Legs and feet.** Leg colour, the scale pattern, claw length.
- **Colour set.** Our own natural colours per region, for example a set drawn from different natural tones than ARK's. The six regions themselves are a function (see [FUNCTIONS.md](FUNCTIONS.md)), so their names and rough placement stay.

## What I could not find out

- Exact sizes. All proportions and the standing height are estimated from pictures.
- A clean, square-on side or front view of either game model. The best side views are in-game screenshots at an angle.
- Whether the ASE model's spot-tipped plume is one feather or two, and exactly where it grows; the official pictures show it at the nape or upper back from different angles.
- Reddit, Pinterest and most Steam artwork pages were out of reach for the fan-art survey, so the sample leans on DeviantArt and the official fan-art features. Curated features may favour pieces close to the game model.
