1. **The hood is feathers, not hair.** In round 2 it reads as a smooth fur helmet, and in the three-quarter view as a human bowl haircut with long straight hair. Paint it as short, dense, soft feathers, visible as small rounded overlapping feathers, smaller and darker than the body's, lying close over the crown and the back of the skull so the skull's shape shows. Around the bare face its edge breaks into a ragged fringe of small tufts, and at the front it comes down to a blunt point on the forehead between the brows. No smooth fur, no helmet, no straight strands, no fringe of bangs.
2. **The nape mane is feathers too.** A short, lank fringe of longer, loose, separate feathers, each with a vane, lying down the back of the neck to about its middle (where the guide ends it), no wider than the neck. Not hair.
3. **The throat pouch is not a ball.** In round 2 it became a round ochre bulb under the chin that reads as a wattle. Paint it as bare, finely pleated ochre skin lying on the front of the neck, barely proud of it: from just under the chin and the corner of the mouth, two thirds of the way down the front of the neck, its pleats running across, its lower edge fading softly into the feathers. 4. **The brow is the heavy shelf of round 1.** A thick, smooth, leathery ochre ridge that starts at the base of the beak and runs back over the eye, jutting forward over it so the eye sits under its overhang. 5. **The face is rose buff, not salmon.** Bare, finely pebbled skin, pale and warm, about #DDA58E as in round 1, with the soft folds under the eye and at the mouth corner. Round 2 drifted toward red-orange.
