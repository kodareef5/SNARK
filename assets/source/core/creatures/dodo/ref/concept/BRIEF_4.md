## What is wrong with the hood

Round 3 turned the hood from hair into feathers, which was the point, but painted them as tiny identical scales in rigidly regular rows, so it reads as a scaly or knitted helmet pulled over the head. Its edge around the face is a clean line, and it has no forehead point. Regular scale-like rows are also exactly what we moved the body away from.

## What the hood should be

- **A plush cap of small soft feathers.** Dense, short, slightly fuzzy contour feathers, each just discernible, overlapping loosely and irregularly, varying a little in size and direction as they follow the skull: smallest and closest on the forehead and crown, a little longer toward the back of the head. Soft light catches the tips. Matte umber, about #3B302A, with slight variation in value so it is not a flat dark shape. No regular rows, no scale pattern, no knit, no smooth fur.
- **A soft outline.** The top and back of the head break into small feather tips against the background, as the body's outline does. Not a smooth dome.
- **A ragged edge around the face.** Where the hood meets the bare rose skin, the feathers end unevenly in small tufts and wisps that overlap the skin, more ragged below and behind the eye. Not a clean line.
- **The forehead point.** At the front the hood comes down between the two brows in a blunt point that reaches toward the top of the beak's base, so from the front the brows sit either side of it and from the side the hood's edge dips toward the beak behind the brow.
- **The nape mane.** Longer loose feathers, each with a vane and a soft tip, lying down the back of the neck to about its middle (where round 3 ends it), slightly separated at their tips so the outline at the back of the neck is lank and a little shaggy. It grows out of the hood without a seam.

The hood stays as close to the skull as in round 3, within the guide silhouettes, the same size and in the same place in every view.
