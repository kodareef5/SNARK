# Paint the beak and the inside of the mouth

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `beak`, your folder `tex/paint/beak/`, and your study `tex/studies/beak/` (accepted).

Your pieces: `beak` (the upper beak), `jaw` (the lower beak), `beak_palate`, `jaw_floor`, `tongue`.

- **`beak` and `jaw`** (Beak region, blue): your accepted horn, painted to this beak. The three raised ring bands circle the upper beak near the face, a few millimetres from where it meets the skin; the long low nostril slit sits on the side about a third of the way along; the ridge runs along the top; toward the hook the horn wears paler and smoother, most at the very tip and along the cutting edges, and the lower beak wears along its edge too. Use the position maps to find where each of these is on this beak (the hook's tip is near (0, 0.50, 0.33) on the bird); your side panel `beak_side` shows where they sit, and the beak piece in the layout is very nearly that side view.
- **`beak_palate`, `jaw_floor`, `tongue`**: the inside of the mouth, seen when the beak opens wide to call or in the startled pose. Never dyed: paint it in its own colour, a dull pink-grey, moist (lower roughness than the horn). The palate has soft transverse ridges; the floor is smooth; the tongue is a little paler and pinker with a fine velvet of papillae and a darker groove down its middle.
