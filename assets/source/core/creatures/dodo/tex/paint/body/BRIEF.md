# Paint the body, the wings and the cream bars

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `body`, your folder `tex/paint/body/`, your study `tex/studies/body/` (accepted), and the trousers study `tex/studies/trousers/` (accepted) for the shaggy lower body.

Your pieces: `shell_body`, `wing_l_out`, `wing_l_in`, `shoulder_l`. Your cards: `body`.

- **`shell_body`** (Body region, red): breast, belly, flanks, back, rump, neck, from the hood's edge and the pouch's edges down. Your accepted feathers lying along the direction field in loose overlapping rows that wrap the body's roundness: smaller on the neck (2.5 to 3 cm), 4 to 4.5 cm on breast and back, longer and looser down the flanks, and at the lower flanks and over the thighs the trousers' long shaggy down (its elements are in `tex/studies/trousers/`), growing out of the body feathers with no line between. The belly's lower edge is soft. Place the feathers in the bird's own space, so the rows run on unbroken across the layout's cuts and are symmetric where they cross the midline.
- **The bars** (region 5, yellow): five rows of narrow cream bars, each the pale tips of one row of feathers, cross the back and rump in chevrons and fade down the flanks; breast and belly are plain. Look at `ref/concept/top.png`, `back.png` and `side.png` for where they lie: they start a little behind the base of the neck, sit about 3 cm apart, point their chevrons toward the tail along the back's midline, and thin and break up as they run down the flanks. Each bar is made of feathers, drawn as your accepted barred feathers are.
- **`wing_l_out`** (red): the folded wing's outer surface, covered in body feathers, smaller toward its leading edge and overlapping toward its back edge, where the four cream fingers (cards) come out.
- **`wing_l_in`** (red): the underside of the wing, which lies against the flank when folded and is seen when the wing is raised in a fall or a flap. Paint it as the soft, pale underside of a wing: short downy feathers, softer, a little paler in value than the outside, not body feathers.
- **`shoulder_l`** (red): small feathers where the wing meets the body.
- **`body` card strip** (roots along the top, tips down): 60 tufts of loose feather tips scattered where the outline runs (back ridge, rump, belly, lower breast, flanks), each drawn from one quarter of the strip's width; a whole strip would span about 100 mm along and 33 mm down on the bird, so a tuft shows about 25 mm of it. Fill it with your loose tips, plain (no bars), so that every quarter holds complete feathers (no feather cut in half at a quarter line) and each quarter is a little different from the others, so the tufts do not read as copies. The cards take the body's normals, so they light as the plumage they sit in: their colour, value and fine drawing must match the body feathers beside them exactly, or they read as patches.

This is the largest and most seen part of the bird: at middle distance it is a round slate body with soft rows and cream chevrons across the back; at arm's length every feather has a shaft and barbs.
