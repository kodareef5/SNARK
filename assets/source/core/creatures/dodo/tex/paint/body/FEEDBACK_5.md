# Body painting, round 5: the tips twice as deep and soft

The coordinator's review of the bird: "at arm's length the bars are white squiggles painted on; each should be a row of pale feather tips, soft at the edges, following the feathers' lie." Measured in the composed 2,048 map (attached: a crop of it, dyed and lit), each pale tip is now a thin crescent 4 to 5 mm deep with a crisp, ragged edge, so a row still reads as a squiggle. In the concept (`concept_bars.png`, attached; `ref/concept/top.png`) each tip is a soft, pointed brush about twice as deep.

Note that the game's software renderer, and so `look.sh`, reads textures at most 1,024 across, half your map; judge the strands in the 2,048 map itself as well, as in the attached crop.

- **Depth:** 8 to 10 mm at the feather's shaft, falling to 2 to 3 mm at the sides, so each tip is a V or a rounded point, not a crescent.
- **Soft:** the cream fades into the grey along the barbs over the inner 4 to 6 mm, strand by strand, with no crisp edge anywhere inside it; the outer edge is the feather's own soft fringe.
- **Following the lie:** each V points the way that feather points (down the back and round the body, as the direction field says), centred on its shaft.
