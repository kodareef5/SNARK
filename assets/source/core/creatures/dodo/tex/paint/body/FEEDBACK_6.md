# Body painting, round 6: accepted

Each bar is now a row of soft pale feather tips, one pointed brush per feather, 8 to 10 mm deep at the shaft and dissolving into the grey along the barbs, breaking up toward the flanks: on the bird it reads as the concept's back, near and far. With the soft rows of the plain plumage, the wing and its underside and the loose tips, the body is accepted as it is.
