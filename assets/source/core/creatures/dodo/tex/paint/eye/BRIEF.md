# Paint the eye and its lids

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `eye`, your folder `tex/paint/eye/`, and your study `tex/studies/eye/` (accepted).

Your pieces: `eye_l`, `lid_l_out`, `lid_l_in`.

- **`eye_l`** is the eyeball: a sphere 12.5 mm in radius centred at (0.043, 0.583, 0.177) on the bird, looking out along (0.70, 0.0, 0.71). Lay your accepted eye on it from the front, along that look direction: the iris 22 mm across with the small 3 mm pupil at its centre, the dark straw margin beyond. Undyed (black in the regions). Smooth and wet.
- **`lid_l_out`** is the outside of the upper lid, now a thick, heavy hood of skin (the modeller made it thicker; this piece grew). Your accepted lid skin: fine pebbles, the uneven folds running along the lid parallel to its edge, heaviest toward the brow, and the smooth thick rim along the lid's free edge, over the eye. Face region (green).
- **`lid_l_in`** is the inside of the lid, seen only when it shuts: smooth, finer, slightly moist skin. Face region.

At the closest the bird is seen, the eye is about 100 pixels across: the pupil must stay a crisp small bead, and the lid's weight is what makes the stare sleepy.
