# Paint the face, the brow and the throat pouch

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `face`, your folder `tex/paint/face/`, and your study `tex/studies/face/` (accepted).

Your pieces: `shell_face`, `brow_l`, `pouch_out`, `pouch_in`.

- **`shell_face`** (Face region, green): the bare pebbled rose skin from the beak's base back round the eye to the hood's edge and down to the corner of the mouth. Pebbles finer toward the eye and the beak, a little larger and flatter on the cheek. Your fold elements laid where the face folds: the three or four heavy rolls in concentric arcs under the eye (the eye's centre is at (0.043, 0.583, 0.177) on the bird; the lower lid line is about 30 degrees below its forward axis), heaviest nearest the eye; the thick down-turned roll at the corner of the mouth (the jowl, near (0.033, 0.543, 0.190)); one or two soft creases behind the eye toward the hood. The part of this piece that is black in the regions is the inside of the mouth behind the beak, seen only when it opens: paint it a dull pink-grey, moist, never dyed.
- **`brow_l`** (Forehead, Neck and Feet region, cyan): the heavy smooth shelf over the eye, with your faint, larger-celled grain, a little smoother along its overhanging front edge.
- **`pouch_out`** (cyan): the flat pleated pouch down the front of the neck from the chin, the pleats running across the throat (square to the neck's length, which the position maps give), about 2 mm apart, crisp, with the odd fork; the pleats fade softly near the pouch's edges, where the neck feathers lie over it.
- **`pouch_in`** (cyan): its hidden inner side: plain, smooth skin.

The face is the most looked-at part of the bird after the beak: at arm's length a player sees each pebble and fold.
