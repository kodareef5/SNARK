First, read `tex/paint/CORRECTION_1.md`: the position maps you painted from held the right side and a clipped crown. Both are fixed; your folds were placed with the old maps, so run your `paint.py` again and check where everything lands.

The skin, the pouch and the brow are right. Two placements are not, set beside the concept in your own `checks/concept_comparison.png` and in the game's renderer (attached):

- **The under-eye rolls belong under the eye.** Yours are a small set of fingerprint arcs behind and below the eye. In the concept (`ref/concept/head_side.png`, `head_front.png`) three or four heavy rolls stack directly beneath the eye, concentric with the lower lid's edge: the first roll lies against the lower edge of the eye itself, each next one a little lower and a little wider, together spanning the eye's whole width (about 25 to 30 mm) and a little beyond at the back, fading at both ends. They are what make the eye look baggy and tired.
- **The mouth-corner roll belongs at the mouth.** Yours is a C on the cheek, behind the beak, reading as an ear. In the concept it is the end of the gape: the line where the upper and lower beak meet runs back into the face and turns down in a thick fleshy roll, so the corner of the mouth droops. Start it where the gape ends at the beak's root and curve it down and back from there, joined to the mouth line.

The mauve patch above the beak's root is the model's region map (never dyed there) and is with the modeller; leave it as you have it.
