# Face painting, round 3: accepted

In the game's renderer the rolls under the eye now read as soft, heavy bags against the lower lid, the corner of the mouth droops from the gape, and the pebbled skin, the brow and the pouch hold up close and at the bird's size. The painting is accepted as it is. More weight in the folds would now have to come from the model's own forms (its jowl and the bag under the eye), not from the maps.
