# Paint the hood, its edge and the mane

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `hood`, your folder `tex/paint/hood/`, and your study `tex/studies/hood/` (accepted).

Your pieces: `shell_hood`. Your cards: `hood`, `forehead`, `mane`.

- **`shell_hood`** (Head region, magenta): your accepted cap over the crown and the back of the head, smallest on the forehead and crown (8 to 12 mm), a little longer toward the back of the head (15 to 20 mm), lengthening without a line into the lank mane down the back of the neck to about its middle (4 to 7 cm). The feathers lie back over the skull and down the nape: follow the direction field. Near the face the hood's own feathers get smaller toward its edge, where the edge cards lie over it.
- **`hood` card strip** (157 mm along, 12 mm deep, roots up): the ragged edge round the face, small tufts of two to five cap feathers hanging over the skin, irregular in length and spacing, their ends breaking into wisps.
- **`forehead` card** (23 by 12 mm): the blunt point of the hood between the brows, a small dense tuft.
- **`mane` card** (78 mm across, 70 mm down, roots up): several long mane feathers side by side, lank, with vanes and soft separated tips.

From across a field the hood is a dark cap; from arm's length it is a soft plush that ends round the face in a ragged edge.
