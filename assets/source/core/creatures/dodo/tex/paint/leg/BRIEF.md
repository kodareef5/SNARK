# Paint the legs, toes and claws

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `leg`, your folder `tex/paint/leg/`, and your study `tex/studies/leg/` (accepted).

Your pieces: `leg_l` and the four toe pieces `toe4_+188`, `toe4_+30`, `toe4_+4`, `toe4_-24` (each toe with its claw).

- **`leg_l`** (Forehead, Neck and Feet region, cyan): the shank from under the trousers to the foot. Large irregular plates over its front half (the side facing forward and out), grading to small pebbly scales round the back. Make the front plates about a third larger than in the study, 10 to 14 mm across, as the acceptance note says; smaller plates round the ankle where the toes leave it. The top of the leg disappears under the trouser cards.
- **The toes** (cyan): plates along the top, pebbles down the sides, rough pads underneath, finer creases at the joints.
- **The claws** (the part of each toe piece that is black in the regions): your accepted claw, dark brown-grey horn at the base to a paler worn tip, polished. Never dyed.
