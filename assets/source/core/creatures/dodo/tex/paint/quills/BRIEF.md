# Paint the tail and finger cards

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `quills`, your folder `tex/paint/quills/`, and your study `tex/studies/quills/` (accepted).

Your cards: `tail` (one tail feather, 35 mm across and 124 mm long, standing root down and tip up in its rectangle) and `finger` (one wing finger, 22 by 56 mm, root down and tip up). All seven tail cards share the one tail picture and all eight fingers share the finger picture, and the model mirrors and curls them, so paint the symmetric central tail feather and your most typical finger, filling each rectangle to the feather's real proportions. The tail feather is body region (red) with its two cream crescent bands near the tip in region 5 (yellow); the finger is all region 5. The cards are seen from both sides.
