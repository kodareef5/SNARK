# Paint the trouser cards

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md` first and follow it. Your group is `trousers`, your folder `tex/paint/trousers/`, and your study `tex/studies/trousers/` (accepted).

Your cards: `trousers` (one strip, roots along the top, hanging down; on the bird it spans about 227 mm along and 59 mm down, used at full width). The cards hang in two rings of long feathers round each thigh, down to the top of the scaly leg, and break the outline there. Fill the strip with your accepted shaggy fringe feathers side by side and overlapping, of irregular length and spacing, so that its lower edge is a ragged fringe of locks and wisps at every point along it, and the strip reads as one soft, shaggy fall wherever the model cuts it. Body region (red), no bars.

The body's painter (`tex/paint/body/`) paints the shaggy lower flanks and thighs on the body itself with your elements; look at theirs when it appears, so the cards and the body beneath them are one fall.
