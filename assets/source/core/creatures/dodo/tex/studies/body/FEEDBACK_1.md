# Body study, round 1: sent back

The numbers are right (mean grey 0.500 in both regions, neutral, normal against height 1.00, seams under 1.1), the sixteen generated feathers with their own layers are what the bird's painter needs, and relit up close the plain feathers have real shafts and soft rounded ends. Your projection onto the concept (`checks/bird_context.png`) was a good idea. But the set is not yet the concept's plumage, so please do it again. The attached sheets are my review: each set relit, dyed, at the bird's size and beside the concept at the concept's scale.

- **The bars are far too wide and do not follow the feathers.** They take 27% of the barred tile, as wavy bands about 12 to 15 mm deep with spiky teeth, so they read as torn paper or grass, not as the pale tips of feathers. In the concept (`ref/concept/top.png`, `back.png`, `side.png`) each bar is a row of separate soft pale tips: on each feather, a narrow crescent of about 4 to 6 mm along its rounded free edge, fringed with barb tips, so the bar is a chain of small scallops or chevrons with body colour showing between and above them. About 8 to 12% of a barred tile, not 27%. The region map's yellow should be exactly those crescents, following each feather's own edge.
- **Shrunk, the plumage is a regular honeycomb.** At the bird's size and at 200 pixels the feathers turn into an even grid of identical scales, which is the pinecone the brief warns against. Make the rows loose: feathers of varied size and width, rows that wave and merge, a crowded pair here and a wider gap there, neighbouring feathers at slightly different angles.
- **The feathers are about half the concept's size.** At the concept's scale yours show about twice as many feathers across as the concept's breast and flank. The visible end of a breast or back feather in the concept is about 4 to 4.5 cm across.
- **The base colour is flat.** Read the new section "Where the drawing lives" in `tex/studies/COMMON.md`. In the concept the rows read at a distance because each feather's exposed end is a little paler than the part tucked under the row above, its fringe is paler still, its shaft is a fine pale line, and the barbs carry fine light and dark. That is value in the base, as well as relief. Keep the mean at 0.50 and the hue at none.
- On the tips sheet, keep a flat, matching margin round the borders (the game's normal-against-height measure wraps and reads 0.89 down), and do not let the height and normal in the transparent gutters fan out in streaks: keep them quiet.

Same sets, names and scales; update the report for this round. Before you stop, set the barred and plain tiles, dyed slate and cream and relit, beside the concept's back and breast at the concept's size: a stranger should see the same soft rows and the same narrow cream chevrons.
