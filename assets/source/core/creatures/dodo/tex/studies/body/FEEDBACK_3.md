# Body study, round 3: the bars are close; one precise correction

The bars are now full-bodied and brushy, and `body_plain` is untouched, as asked. One thing is still unlike the concept, and it matters, because it is the scale-like look the design moves away from: in your comparison (`checks/round3_bars_comparison.png`) the cream runs right round the exposed edge of each feather and up both of its sides, so every feather is **outlined** in cream, like a scale with a pale rim.

In the concept (left column of that same picture) the pale part is only at the bottom of each feather's tip:

- **Deepest at the shaft**, about 8 to 10 mm, as a soft pointed brush or V of pale barbs centred on the shaft line.
- **Thinning to nothing** about two thirds of the way out toward each side. It never climbs the sides of the feather; the sides stay body colour.
- **Neighbours' tips meet** at their lower corners, so along a row the pale brushes join into one continuous zigzag (a chain of Vs), with body colour above and between them.
- The inner edge stays brushy, as you have it now; the outer edge is the feather's own fringe.

Coverage will fall back somewhat, to perhaps 12 to 15%. Same for the barred elements of `body_tips`. Keep `body_plain` byte for byte. Same names and scale; update the report and finish with the same comparison.
