# Body study, round 5: the right structure; one last adjustment, then paint the bird

This is the right structure at last: each barred row is one continuous band of pale barb ends that follows the feathers. Set it beside the concept once more (your comparison): the concept's band is about twice as deep and much softer.

- **Deeper and pointed at each shaft.** At each feather's centre the pale ends reach 8 to 10 mm up the feather (a soft V pointing down the body, centred on the shaft), thinning to 2 or 3 mm between feathers. Yours are 3 to 5 mm everywhere, so the band reads as a thin zigzag line.
- **Soft, not piped.** The pale strands fade into the grey along their length, the inner edge a comb of strands of different lengths; and the relief of a pale tip is the feather's own, no higher than the rest of the vane. Yours read as raised beads of frosting under light: lower the pale tips' relief to the vane's and drop the hard rim at their edge.

Make that change to `body_barred` and the barred elements of `body_tips`, keep `body_plain` byte for byte, and check it beside the concept. Then go straight on: your study is accepted with that change.
