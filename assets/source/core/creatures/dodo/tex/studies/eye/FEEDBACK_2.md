# Eye study, round 2: the eye is accepted; the lid goes back

**The eye is right.** A small 3 mm pupil in a plain olive-straw iris, fine radial fibres, a few crypts, a darker limbal ring: at the concept's size it is the concept's dim stare. Keep `eye` exactly as it is.

**The lid has gone from ripples to tubes.** Your lid shell in `checks/concept_comparison.png` looks like a pink inflatable ring next to the concept's lid. The three folds are identical smooth sausages, evenly spaced, perfectly parallel, of one width and one depth, and the skin over them has lost its pebbling. Look at the concept (`ref/concept/head_side.png`, `head_front.png`) once more:

- **Folds of skin, not tubes.** The lid's folds differ from one another: the one nearest the brow is the heaviest and droops most; the others are thinner and shallower; they taper and fade at different points, one may split or merge into its neighbour, and the creases between them are of different depths. Fleshy, uneven, a little asymmetric.
- **Pebbled all over.** The concept's lid is the face's pebbled skin, finer: small cells about 0.5 to 1 mm that you can see on the folds and in the creases, with the creases darker. In round 2 the pebbling is a faint leathery grain that disappears at the bird's size. Make it a real fine pebbling, in the height and in the base.
- **The rim** is good as a smooth, thick edge; keep it.
- The panel is one lid long and its folds fade toward the corners, so it does not truly tile across; say so in `study.json` rather than claiming it tiles, and keep a flat, matching margin at the top and bottom borders (the game's normal-against-height measure wraps and reads 0.70 down).
