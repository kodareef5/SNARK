Use case: stylized-concept. Production raster source atlas for a realistic bird feather material.
Create TWELVE DIFFERENT single tiny soft contour feathers, shown individually much enlarged, arranged strictly four columns by three rows, each isolated in its equal cell on genuinely transparent background. Each feather root at top, tips pointing down. Leave generous clear space between feathers. No labels or frames.

Each is a little elongated soft tuft: dark neutral grey at its root and central down, medium grey main vane with very fine pale grey individual barbs sweeping downwards, subtly paler frayed fuzzy terminal tips. Strong enough natural fine value texture to remain visible when reduced. Finely branched organic soft strands gathered into 5–9 tapering little locks, irregularly split tips, wispy fringe with deeply separated small groups of barbs at the lower third, NO single smooth enclosing leaf outline. Soft down nestles beneath the feather but avoid a round ball of fluff. Thin subtle shaft almost hidden. Asymmetric silhouette, shallow natural curl. These are all the SAME neutral grey material with modest variation; no hue, no stripes, no white rim. Detailed macro rendering, soft even omnidirectional illumination, no cast shadows or directional sheen. Plush tactile microgeometry, delicate strands but substantial opaque strand bundles, not transparent ghost feathers. Do not draw a bird, surface patch, fur, scales, knit, leaves, quills, or plant veins. The painter will overlap these into a dense cap. Keep the roots compact and the tips irregular, soft and tapering. Square atlas.
