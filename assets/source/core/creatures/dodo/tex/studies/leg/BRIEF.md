# Leg study — round 3 brief

Follow `../COMMON.md`, including **Where the drawing lives**. This revision
supersedes the narrow plate stripe and weak claw gradient in round two.

The whole front half of the shank is plated with unequal 6–10 mm scutes, grading
through 3–5 mm side plates into irregular 1.5–3 mm scales at the back. Use broad,
near-square, five-sided and wedge-shaped plates of varied proportions, staggered
without a lattice. Avoid repeated hexagons and uniform bead-like rear scales.
Toes are plated over tops and sides, with rougher pads underneath. Preserve the
firm gently domed faces, soft rims, fine grain and dark intrinsic crease pigment
that worked in round two. Dye bases remain neutral, mean 0.50 linear, region 3 cyan.

The undyed claw runs clearly from dark brown-grey at its root (about sRGB 70) to
paler worn grey horn at its tip (about 130), with a soft sheen and a few faint
longitudinal growth lines. Relief must be smooth, not noise. Region map all black.
