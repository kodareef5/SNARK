# Leg study, round 3: accepted

The shank and toes are now plated the way the concept's are: irregular, slightly domed plates of many shapes and sizes, a fine leathery grain on each, clean dark creases between, grading to small scales round the back with no line; and they read as a plated leg in every colour of the set. The claw runs from dark brown-grey at the base to a paler worn tip, smooth and polished. All three sets are accepted as they are.

For the bird: the concept's front plates are a little larger than these (about 10 to 14 mm across the front of the shank, against 6 to 10 here). When the bird's legs are painted, scale the plates up by about a third with the build's scale setting.
