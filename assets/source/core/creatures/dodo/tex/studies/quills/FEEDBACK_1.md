# Quills study, round 1: sent back

The numbers are right (mean grey 0.500 in both regions, neutral, normal against height 1.00), the sheet is laid out cleanly with its boxes, roots and tips, and your assembled fan and fingers (`checks/card_assemblies.png`) are a good way to judge cards. But the feathers are not yet the concept's, so please do the sheet again. The attached sheets are my review, and `concept_tail.png` is the concept's tail and wing enlarged.

- **The tail feathers are too narrow and pointed.** The concept's tail feathers are broad and soft, about 3 cm wide over most of their length, with broad, rounded tips and a soft fringed edge, so that the seven of them make a full, rounded fan. Yours taper like quill pens.
- **The bands are cut straight across, like tape.** In the concept each band is a crescent that follows the rounded tip: two narrow cream arcs, concentric with the tip's edge, with body colour between them and a body-coloured margin at the very tip. Their edges are soft where the barbs carry them, and the region map follows those arcs. Yours are straight strips with saw-toothed edges, and on the region map large straight-cut polygons.
- **The base colour is flat.** Read the new section "Where the drawing lives" in `tex/studies/COMMON.md`. A feather's shaft is paler, its barbs carry fine light and dark, and its vane is a little darker toward the shaft and lighter toward the fringe. Draw that in the base, mean 0.50, no hue.
- **The cut-out edges are saw teeth.** At the sheet's size and at a quarter of it, the edges should be soft clumps of barb tips, not regular serrations.
- The fingers are close: broad, soft and cream with a clear shaft. Give them the same value drawing and soft fringed edges, and round their tips a little more, as in the concept.

Same sheet, names and scale; update the report for this round. Before you stop, assemble the fan and the fingers dyed slate and cream, relit, beside the concept's tail and wing at the concept's size: a stranger should see the same rounded, banded fan.
