# Quills study, round 2: nearly there, one more push on the edges

The shapes are right now: broad tail feathers with rounded tips that make a full fan, two cream crescents concentric with each tip and a body-coloured margin, broad soft fingers, and at the bird's card size (`checks/default_slate_512.png`) the fan and the fingers read cleanly in every colour. Keep the shapes, the bands, the layout and the scale.

What is left is close up, where a player holding the bird will see it:

- **The outlines are paper-cut.** At full size the tail feathers and fingers have clean, smooth outlines with a few saw-notches near their bases. In the concept (`concept_tail.png`, attached again) every edge is soft: the vane ends in a fringe of fine barb tips all the way round, fluffiest at the rounded tip, and here and there a small split where the barbs part. The cut-out at 0.5 should follow those barb tips, so the edge is a fine fringe at full size and still soft, not smooth, at a quarter size.
- **The vanes are too smooth.** The barbs are faint, so a feather reads as a smooth leaf. Give the barbs more presence in the base and the height: fine parallel strands sweeping out from the shaft, softly grouped, catching the light, as in the concept's tail.
- **The tail's shaft** is a fine dark line in the concept; yours is pale. Either reads, but a slightly darker, crisper shaft would help the fan read as seven feathers at a distance.
