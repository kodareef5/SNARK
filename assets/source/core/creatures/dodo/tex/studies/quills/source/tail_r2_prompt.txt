Use case: photorealistic-natural. Reusable texture source for a flightless dodo's soft tail feather cards. Transparent RGBA background.
Make a landscape contact sheet of exactly FOUR individual neutral grey feathers, well separated in four equal columns, all full length, no labels, no shadows on background. ALL narrow exposed ROOT SHAFTS AT THE TOP, broad rounded terminal TIPS AT THE BOTTOM. First feather perfectly symmetric and straight; next three have subtly asymmetric vanes and gently curved shafts, increasingly curved. Each feather 11 cm long and 3 cm wide: a BROAD PADDLE with almost parallel sides from 25% to 85% of its length, ending in a broad HALF-CIRCLE rounded cap occupying the last 15%. NOT pointed, NOT lanceolate, NOT a writing quill. Short root stalk then widen promptly to the full vane width. The bottom ends must be blunt and rounded, like a wide soft dodo tail feather.
Macro photographic soft feather structure: a pale slender visible rachis, long sweeping diagonal barbs down and out from it, coherent silky fine filaments with small light and dark value differences. A little darker beside the shaft, lighter toward the soft fringe. Grey pigment only, NO BANDS: the two colored bands will be applied separately. No hue. Smooth broad illumination like a flatbed specimen scan, no specular highlight or directional shading. Soft fine clumped barb tips naturally break the silhouette, irregular delicate fringe, no regular saw teeth, no deep tears, no serrated leaf edges. Keep feather tips well clear of the frame. Soft real vanes, not embossed clay, no woodgrain or leaf veins. High resolution detail, generous transparent space between feathers.
