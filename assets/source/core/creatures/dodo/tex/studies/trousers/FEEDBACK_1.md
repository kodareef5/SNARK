# Trousers study, round 1: sent back

The numbers are right (mean grey 0.500, neutral, normal against height 1.00, seams under 1.07), the twelve generated feathers in `source/` are lovely, exactly the soft, wispy, shafted down the design asks for, and keeping each one's layers separately is what the bird's painter needs. But the delivered layers lost what makes those feathers good, so please do the sets again. The attached sheet is my review; your own `checks/trousers_slate_full.png` shows the same thing.

- **The base colour is flat.** Dyed and shown unlit it is an even grey with nothing in it: the barbs, the shaft, the locks and the gaps between them are all gone, because the fine light and dark of your originals was removed along with their light. At the bird's size and under soft light the trousers are therefore a blank grey. Read the new section "Where the drawing lives" in `tex/studies/COMMON.md`: the fine value structure of the feathers (light strands, darker gaps between locks, a paler shaft near the root) stays in the base. Only the originals' broad light goes.
- **The relief reads as plaster.** Relit, the tile looks like a grey wall with squiggles carved into it, or wet clay, not down. Each feather's height is a smooth extruded silhouette with steep, cut edges, and between feathers there are deep holes. Down has almost no surface and no edges: its relief should be many fine strands (the barbs, as fine ridges) over a gentle rounding of each lock, with soft steps where one feather lies over the next, and the fall dense and layered enough that no gap ever opens onto background, only onto deeper strands.
- **The fringe's cut edge is serrated, like leaves.** Cut at 0.5, the outlines are smooth lobes with saw-teeth; the separate strands at the edges of your originals are gone. The cut-out edge should be clumps of strands, as in the originals, and still read as wispy at 512 for the sheet.
- Minor: the game's own normal-against-height measure wraps the picture, and on the fringe sheet it reads 0.79 down because the top and bottom borders disagree. Keep a flat, matching margin round all four borders of a sheet.

Look at the concept's lower body (`ref/concept/side.png`, around the top of the leg) at the concept's scale beside yours, and at your originals beside your delivered set at the same size. When the delivered set has everything the originals have, and reads as the concept's soft, shaggy trousers at the bird's size, it is done.
