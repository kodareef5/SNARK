# Saddle, round 1: three things sent back

Read `tex/paint/BRIEF.md`, your `BRIEF.md` and your `REPORT.md` again, then this. Most of the saddle is right and
stays: the seat's oiled russet hide with its stitched rolled edge, the straps and their stitch lines, the plaited reins
and foot-rest ropes, the twisted halter, the wrapped grips, the pegs. The attached picture is the game's renderer from
in front and a little above (`saddled_front`), enlarged.

- **The cantle's grain runs the wrong way.** On the bent-wood bow the grain crosses the bow in short stripes, so it
  reads as wrapped cord or a woven band. Bent wood's grain runs along the bend, from one end of the bow to the other.
  Turn it on `cantle` (check the piece's direction against the position maps rather than the layout's).
- **The chitin reads as white plastic.** The cantle's plate and the boss are a smooth, glossy, near-white with a cold
  bluish rim, like porcelain or a plastic button. The study's chitin is bone to ivory, warmer and a little darker,
  waxy rather than glossy, with faint growth layers following the plate's edge and a deeper, slightly translucent tone
  where it is worn at its edges. Bring that through on both faces of the plate and on the boss, at the study's
  roughness or a little rougher, so it reads as something that grew.
- **The boss is a pearl.** Beyond its colour, make it read as a shaped piece of chitin set where the straps cross: its
  growth layers ringing it, a worn, slightly paler crown, a darker line where it sits in the hide.

The white, jagged patches where the breast straps cross over the chest look like the fuzz showing through the straps:
if that is geometry (the straps passing into the body), say so in your report for the modeller and leave it; if it is
anything of yours, fix it. Everything else as it is. Update the report for this round, and look at `saddled_front`,
`saddled_seat` and `saddled_rider` before you stop.
