# Membrane study, round 2: accepted

The membrane is skin now: tapering trunks that branch and rejoin, the fine skin between them, creases where it
gathers, and in the game's renderer the veins read at 0.5 and 1.5 m in the design's slate and fall into the tone at
5 m. Accepted as it is; do not change any delivered picture.

Two notes for the animal's painter, not for you: on the animal the membrane has about 600 texels a metre, a third of
this study's density, so its vessels will be drawn bolder there to stay two texels wide; and from below the game lights
the under face by the sky below alone, so it reads darker than the concept whatever its base. That is the engine's to
weigh, not the texture's.
