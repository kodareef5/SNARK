# Skin study, round 1: four sets accepted, the eye sent back

Read `tex/studies/COMMON.md`, your `BRIEF.md` and your `REPORT.md` again, then this. `skin`, `scutes`, `claw` and
`mouth` are accepted as they are: the pebbles are real pebbles, the scutes sit in bands with the pebbles between them,
the claw is a dark polished hook with a worn point, and the numbers are right. Do not change them. (Until now
`look.ts` drew tubes and eggs inside out; it is fixed, and on the fixed renderer those four read as they should at
their small size.)

The iris is good: dark amber, fine radial fibres, a round black pupil, no painted catchlight. Keep it. What is round it
is not yet the concept's eye:

- **The skin round the eye is a lace pattern.** Outside the lid you drew a wide field of bare skin with scalloped,
  ornamental wrinkles that ring the eye like a doily. In the concept (`ref/concept/head_side.png`, `head_front.png`)
  the eye sits in the charcoal streak of fuzz: a thin lid rim of bare, finely wrinkled skin a millimetre or two wide,
  then the face's short fuzz begins and lies back from the eye toward the nape. Draw that: the lid rim, a narrow ring
  of bare skin at most 2 to 3 mm wide, and short fuzz out to the panel's edge, lying left to right (from the beak
  toward the nape, the beak being to the left). The fuzz study (`tex/studies/fuzz/`) is painting the face's fuzz now;
  you may read its pictures and match their character, never write there.
- **The slight brow.** Above the eye the design has a slight brow: a soft fold of skin and fuzz that overhangs the
  upper lid a little and shades it. Give it to the panel in the height, with the upper lid a little in its shadow in
  the occlusion, not in the base.

Keep the region map's rule (black on the eye and the lid rim's inside edge, red on the lid's skin and round it), the
panel's size and scale. Update the report for this round.
