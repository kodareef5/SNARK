# Second round: two curtains

Read `common.md` in this folder first: it is the brief your first round was painted to (what an element is, what to deliver for each, how it is judged). The first round's `drape-0` and `drape-1` are in this folder as you delivered them. Everything else from that round was accepted.

What the game does with a curtain: it hangs 12 cm behind a window's glass, drawn to the sides, and the game reads only its base colour and alpha (it is lit as part of the dim room behind the glass, not by the sun on the wall). So its folds must be in the base colour.

What is wrong: both read as flat sheets of paper at 3 m and 15 m. A curtain is soft vertical folds.

Paint both again, same sizes (256 by 512, 1.0 m by 2.0 m, tiling left to right) and same files:
- In the base colour, gentle vertical folds 8 to 15 cm apart, irregular in width and depth, as the darker inside of each fold and a lighter crest, about 25% darker in a fold than on a crest; the hem's folds splay a little. This is the one element whose shading is in its colour, because the game has no light of its own for it. No cast shadow across it and no light from one side: the folds are lit evenly from the front.
- `drape-0`: the sun-bleached floral print, gone almost to beige, kept; no motif readable at 15 m. `drape-1`: plain faded blue-grey, kept. Keep the ragged hem (alpha 0 below it) and a tear or two.
- Height: the fold profile, 40 mm; normal from it; the rest as before.
- Check it tiles left to right with no seam (`checks/offset.png`) and look at it on `checks/sheet.png` at 1/8: the folds still show as vertical banding.
Write `REPORT.md` for this round.
