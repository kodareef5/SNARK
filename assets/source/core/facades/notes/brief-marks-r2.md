## Second round of marks: eight more posters, five more large pieces, and remains of window vinyl

Read `marks-r1.md` in this folder first: it is the brief the first round of marks was painted to (what a mark is, what to deliver, the writing rule, how it is judged); everything it says holds here. `existing-marks.png` shows what that round made. A street now shows the same few too often: the new ones must each look unlike those and unlike each other.

Deliver each in a folder named as given, `base.png` only, 8-bit RGBA, sRGB, exactly the size given (256 pixels a metre), alpha 0 wherever the wall or the glass shows. Then `REPORT.md` and `checks/sheet.png` as in `marks-r1.md`, with the vinyls shown over dark glass (a dark blue-grey) instead of the wall.

Writing: only the words given below; no other words, names or numbers; no real brand, shop, venue, band, place of business or person; no people, faces or bodies in any picture.

- `poster-7`: "MUSICA EN VIVO" and "VIERNES", with a simple drawn guitar and palm (no people); faded purple and orange.
- `poster-8`: "PERRO PERDIDO" big, a simple ink drawing of a short-haired brown dog, "CANELA" under it.
- `poster-9`: "EVACUATION ROUTE" over a big arrow pointing left; printed white on faded green.
- `poster-10`: "FERIA DE BARRIO" and "DOMINGO", with drawn bunting and a simple sun; faded yellow and blue.
- `poster-11`: "CURFEW 9 PM" bold in black on faded orange, "BY ORDER" small under it.
- `poster-12`: "SE ALQUILA" big, "LOCAL" under it; red stencil letters on white card, its corners held by yellowed tape.
- `poster-13`: "NO TRESPASSING" printed black on white inside a red border.

### Remains of window vinyl, 307 by 205 pixels (1.2 m by 0.8 m)

Self-adhesive vinyl stuck on the inside of a shop's display glass, now sun-rotted, crazed and mostly peeled. Alpha is how much of the glass the vinyl covers: 0 for clear glass, partial where it is thin, frosted or half lifted. Its RGB is the vinyl's colour. Seen over the dark of an empty shop behind the glass.
- `vinyl-0`: the remains of white cut-vinyl letters "ABIERTO" over three rows of short bars standing for opening hours (bars, not letters or numbers); about a third of the letters peeled away, leaving faint ghosts of adhesive (low alpha) where they were.
- `vinyl-1`: a faded rectangular frame line of peeled vinyl, about 3 cm wide with rounded corners, a stretch of it gone, and a ghost of adhesive where it was.
- `vinyl-2`: a band of frosted privacy film 0.5 m high across the lower part of the picture with a scalloped top edge, crazed into small flakes, a third of them gone (alpha 0); the rest of the picture alpha 0.
