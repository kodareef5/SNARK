## Checks

- Reopened all 20 exports and checked the PNG headers. Each file is 8-bit RGBA with an sRGB chunk. Posters are 154 × 230 px, tags 256 × 128 px, pieces 820 × 308 px and garage marks 154 × 205 px.
- Checked zero and intermediate alpha values in every decal. The standalone marks have transparent outermost rows and columns. The column-foot band reaches both sides and the bottom, with rows 0 through 140 fully transparent and paint confined to the final 64 rows.
- Inspected the lettering, illustrations, paper holes and spray edges in composites. The proof wall is supplied by the sheet. The standalone PNGs contain only paper or pigment.
- Compared the set at one-sixth and one-twenty-fourth scale on charcoal, mid-grey and pale backgrounds. Also placed five representative decals over the supplied street image. These were image-space checks. Actual camera distances, mip filtering and game lighting remain unverified.

## Where detail falls away

TREVA's thin blue strokes are the first to lose definition on mid-grey. The boxing gloves become two joined red shapes once the bill is about 30 pixels tall. On the darkest facade, the final A in MAREA is harder to pick out than the other letters because so much wall shows through it.
