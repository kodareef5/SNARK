## Window vinyl

I built the vinyls from cut shapes and fracture masks at four times delivery resolution. The RGB channels carry the vinyl or adhesive colour. Alpha carries the coverage over the glass. Each export measures 307 × 205 pixels.

| Folder | Construction and measurements |
| --- | --- |
| `vinyl-0` | Slanted white ABIERTO above three rows of short cut bars. Irregular peel boundaries leave faint adhesive across 33.5% of the original letter area. Fine cracks continue through the surviving strokes. |
| `vinyl-1` | A rounded frame with a nominal strip width of 7.68 pixels, equivalent to 3 cm. A long peel runs from the top across the upper-right corner and partway down the side. A shorter gap breaks the lower-left stretch. Low-alpha residue traces both gaps. |

## Checks and limits

- Reopened all sixteen PNGs and checked their dimensions, 8-bit RGBA headers, sRGB chunks, clear pixels, and partial-alpha edges. Each decal folder contains only `base.png`.
- Read the poster copy and piece lettering against the brief, checked the arrow direction and three rows of hours bars, and inspected the illustrations for stray writing or figures.
- Compared the new designs with the first-round sheet. Inspected the native and one-eighth composites, plus quarter-scale proofs on warm light and cool dark surfaces. At one twenty-fourth scale the posters read primarily by colour and silhouette.
- MANGLAR’s inner joins merge on mid-grey at one eighth scale. The peeled frame corner also becomes very faint on dark glass. Those are the two details most sensitive to viewing distance. The distance checks used static composites. In-game lighting and texture filtering remain unverified.
