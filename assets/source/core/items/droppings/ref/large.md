# Large dropping references

- **Large.** Karora’s public-domain cowpat has a broad skirt, an off-center crown and folds that sweep around the crest. The pickup keeps the shallow, overlapping folds across a roughly 0.4 m footprint. [Cowpat](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cowpat.jpg)
- **Massive.** Melanurya’s public-domain photograph of wild elephant dung shows a round bolus with flattened shoulders and a fibrous edge. The pickup stacks five rounded boluses at two heights to form a roughly 0.7 m heap with deep seams. [Elephant dung near Munnar](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Elephant_dung.jpg)
