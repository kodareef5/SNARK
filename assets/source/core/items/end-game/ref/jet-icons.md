# Wraith onboard icons

The icons show three original exterior objects for the fictional XF-51 Wraith. The SNARK `next-50` inventory contact sheet supplied the visual reference. The built-in ImageGen tool generated one transparent PNG per object. The final PNGs use a centered 128×128 RGBA canvas.

A rounded vented Wraith pod. The SNARK silencer and laser icons inform its muted blue-gray metal and teal accent.

The built-in source is `exec-8748d1f0-f29d-4ebc-9196-5e19f81b80e8.png`. The final image was cropped around the generated alpha and resized to fit a 100-pixel square.
