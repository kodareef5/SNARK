# Electric prod

The DuraProd product photograph shows a deep rubber grip at one end of a long,
slim insulated stem. That shape informed the silhouette. [Miller Manufacturing, DuraProd](https://miller-mfg.com/products/duraprod-electric-livestock-prod-with-rh36)

SNARK's fictional prop uses a flared rubber palm rest, a tapered amber stem and a
wide rubber nose with two blunt steel pads. The two pads and the gap between
them carry the silhouette at distance. The mesh represents the exterior only.
