# Pike and sword references

- **Pike.** The Met's Pike, ca. 1700, has a slim ridge down the leaf, a bulb below it and a long bell-shaped collar above the wooden shaft. The game prop widens the leaf and collar so the steel reads at a distance. A dark hide grip and a capped butt break up the straight wooden shaft. [The Metropolitan Museum of Art, 14.25.232](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/26676)
- **Sword.** Cleveland's Sword, ca. 1350, has a shallow central groove, straight crossguard and round brass pommel. The game prop takes those outlines into a broad taper with a rounded tip, a brass guard with swept ends and a dark hide grip. [Cleveland Museum of Art, 1923.1121](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1923.1121)
