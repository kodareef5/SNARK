# Early hand tools

The meshes use original forms and materials. The collection pages show the everyday shapes.

- The torch uses a crooked stem, pale folded collar and a swollen charred crown. The collection object shows the long, relatively bare wooden stem and darkened end. [Pine Knot Torch, Smithsonian National Museum of American History](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_872611)
- The slingshot uses uneven prongs, a shallow hanging ribbon and a dark hide pouch across an open fork. The collection object shows the branch asymmetry and the distinct pouch between the bands. [Slingshot – Tree Branch, Museums Victoria](https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/254011)
- The club uses a swept wooden head, a short neck and a broad hide grip. The collection object shows the contrast between a narrow shaft and a rounded head. [Club (Knobkerrie), Cleveland Museum of Art](https://www.clevelandart.org/print/art/2010.203)
