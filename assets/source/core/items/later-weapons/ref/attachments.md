# Dropped attachments

The four props use original game geometry. The references informed their exterior shapes and colour blocks.

- The scope takes its long silhouette and broad end from a prospect glass. Two unequal bells and a raised brass saddle distinguish the game profile. [One-draw prospect glass, Science Museum Group](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co4129/telescope-prospect-glass-spyglass-1-draw-tube)
- The silencer uses the long case and end accents of a metal pen case. Its game profile has a plain dark body and two solid steel ends. [Pen Case with Inkwell, LACMA](https://collections.lacma.org/object/32103)
- The flashlight takes its flared head and contrasting grip from a PolyTac X. Its profile uses a broad steel flare and a simple, dark handle. [PolyTac X, Streamlight](https://www.streamlight.com/products/detail/polytac-x)
- The laser takes its flat optic and faceted casing from a W4S-3 Inox sensor. A broad steel bracket contrasts with the dark shell and makes the underside easy to spot. [W4S-3 Inox photoelectric sensors, SICK](https://www.sick.com/media/docs/3/13/913/product_information_miniature_photoelectric_w4s_3_inox_and_w4s_3_inox_hygiene_en_im0043913.pdf)
