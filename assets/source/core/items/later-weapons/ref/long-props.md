# Long prop references

- The Winchester Model 1897 suggests a broad wooden forearm below a slim metal spine. SNARK's pump prop exaggerates the scallops and thick heel, with warm wood, charcoal iron and a rubber pad on both flat levels. [Smithsonian National Museum of American History, Winchester Model 1897](https://www.si.edu/object/winchester-model-1897-slide-action-riot-shotgun%3Anmah_417434)
- The Sturmgewehr 44 suggests a compact silhouette with the magazine breaking the lower edge. SNARK's assault prop uses a sharp forward dogleg, a sloping rear wedge, molded panels and a dark rubber heel. [National Army Museum, MP 44, 1996-08-248-1](https://collection.nam.ac.uk/detail.php?acc=1996-08-248-1)
- The Lee-Enfield L42A1 suggests a long stock beneath a raised scope. SNARK's sniper prop makes the stock's comb a single sweep and puts a broad colored lens hood over a slender front. [Australian War Memorial, Lee-Enfield L42A1, REL/16987.001](https://collection.awm.gov.au/C306232)

The meshes use original exterior shapes. The material maps carry the fine wear and fastener marks.
