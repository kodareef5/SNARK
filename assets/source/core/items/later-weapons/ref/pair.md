# Fabricated pistol and compound bow

- **Fabricated pistol.** The Armémuseum Pistol m/88 side view shows a straight slide, a visible step to the frame, and a grip that angles back below the hand. SNARK gives its slide about a third of the prop's height and uses bright chamfered steel, a dark muzzle recess, an angular guard and a flared heel. [Armémuseum, Pistol m/88 (AM.090915)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Glock_17_Swedish_Armed_Forces_AM.090915_(0).jpg)
- **Compound bow.** The Mathews ARC 30 product silhouette suggests a forward curve with dark limbs and a cam visible at each end. SNARK uses a steel frame that bends clear of the palm, open braces, and oversize brass ovals in two different shapes. The ovals carry the silhouette at the far levels. [Mathews Archery, ARC 30](https://mathewsinc.com/products/arc-30)

Both props use original exterior geometry. The references informed the silhouette.
