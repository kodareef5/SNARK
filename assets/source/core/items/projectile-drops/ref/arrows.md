# Grounded arrows

The models take the tapered wooden shaft, split nock and three radial vanes from the
[existing flight arrow](../../weapons/thrown/model.py). The collection pieces inform the exterior shapes.

- The stone arrow uses a chipped flint head with a wide shoulder, a hide binding and low shield-shaped vanes. The Late Jōmon point shows an irregular triangular outline and broad faces. [Arrowhead, The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/62428)
- The tranquilizer arrow uses a squat green capsule with angled shoulders, pale brass ends and tall, swept green vanes. The Roman bottle shows a biconical body, raised rim and green glass. [Glass bottle, The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/249413)
- The flame arrow uses a flared orange wrap with a sloped lip, a dark iron tip and long pennant vanes. The wall bracket shows a torch-shaped stem that flares at the top. [Three-light wall brackets, The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/206585)
- The metal arrow uses a long steel leaf with a central ridge, an iron collar and clipped iron vanes. The ceremonial piece shows the willow-leaf outline. [Arrowhead by Seijiro, The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/23565)
