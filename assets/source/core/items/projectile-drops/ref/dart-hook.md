# Dart and grappling hook pickups

- **Tranquilizer dart.** The Pneu-Dart Type C catalog photograph shows a slender metal body interrupted by a bright tail. The SNARK pickup keeps the brass barrel and silver collar of the authored flying dart. Short orange strips flare into a broad fringe that reads from above. [Pneu-Dart, Tranquilizer Darts](https://www.pneudart.com/tranquilizer-darts/)
- **Grappling hook.** The Rijksmuseum Boarding Grapnel suggests an open suspension ring and swept arms with a gap between them. SNARK uses three broad iron flukes, a silver eye and brighter fluke ends. The spread arms and the hole in the eye carry the silhouette at a distance. [Rijksmuseum, NG-MC-1019](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/object/Grappling-hook--6190be9cf3f9105511d7bbd77bf2930f)

The meshes use original exterior geometry. The linked records provide visual reference.
