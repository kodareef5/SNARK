# Grounded rocket

The pickup takes its narrow neck, short tail vanes and broad front casing from the
[existing flight model](../../weapons/launcher/model.py). The tin toy supplies a visual reference
for the distinct blunt tip. [Moon Rocket, Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum](https://www.si.edu/object/toy-tin-toy-moon-rocket%3Anasm_A20060092000)

The raised orange band separates the green casing from the narrower steel tail. The band,
blunt point and four vanes stay in the far silhouette.
