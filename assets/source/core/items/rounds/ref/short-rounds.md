# Compact round pickups

Three inert props use original silhouettes shaped for ground readability. The collection
pieces inform their exterior shapes.

- The pistol pickup has a silver dome above a broad brass body. A dark band cuts into its heel. The inkwell pairs a cylindrical body with a domed top. [Inkwell with Floral and Animal Imagery, The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/450406)
- The shotgun pickup has the widest outline, with an orange paper body between a brass heel and a flat, dark mouth. The pyxis shows a broad cylindrical wall. [Cylindrical Box (Pyxis), The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/448443)
- The advanced pistol pickup carries a pointed silver front above a narrow dark step and a long green body. The bottle shows the abrupt shoulder and slender neck. [Glass bottle with indented side, The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/249447)
