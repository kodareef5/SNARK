# Compact round pickups

The three pickups use cartridge exteriors as silhouette references and keep their game scale.

- The pistol uses the .45 Auto's straight case and broad, rounded nose. The brass case and silver nose meet at a low step. [Malis's .45 Auto photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:45_ACP_-_FMJ_-_SB_-_1.jpg)
- The shotgun uses a 12-gauge shell's raised brass rim and straight hull. The orange body ends in a dark, recessed face. [Wikidudeman's 12-gauge shell photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:12gaugeshotgunshell.jpg)
- The advanced pistol uses the FN 5.7×28's short shoulder, narrow neck and pointed nose. The green case sets it apart from the brass pistol pickup. [Metroplex's FN 5.7×28 photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:5point7x28mm-3cartridges.png)
