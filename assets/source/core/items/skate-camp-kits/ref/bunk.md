# Camp bunk bed

The timber bunk follows the two-tier bed in the Government Mineral Springs Guard Station
in Skamania County, Washington. Photograph 26 shows a spare wood frame, a low mattress
and a plank across the upper berth. [National Register of Historic Places nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/b140f952-cb84-47d5-b804-6f40d1273e4c)

The game bunk adds a ladder at the foot-side opening and leaves room between the two
1.91 m mattresses. The materials match the simple bed's wood rails, hide cover and linen.
The smallest mesh combines each mattress and cover into one shape and keeps the ladder
and upper guards open.
