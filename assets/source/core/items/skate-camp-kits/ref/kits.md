# Packed vehicle assemblies

The ground props use original geometry and shared game materials. These compact packages represent the items carried in inventory.

- The street skateboard packs two transverse trucks over a maple blank. The deck curls at both ends and the paired dark wheels sit outside the transport wraps. The real board's length, double kicks and two trucks set the proportions. [Innerscene street skateboard](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/skateboard-a7585732)
- The motorboat kit rides on an open wooden crate with a rolled bundle across the stern. The exposed steel panels show a rising pointed bow and flared gunwales. The reference supplies precut hull plates, preformed bow sections and drawings in a freight crate. [Alu Kit Boats](https://www.alukitboats.com/faq.html)
- The helicopter kit uses a long, narrow hard case with two reinforced bands. A short two-blade tail rotor sits across the lid and shows the central hub and clipped blade ends. The reference uses long fitted crates to protect rotor blades during transport. [Van Horn Aviation rotor blade shipping](https://www.vanhornaviation.com/2021/05/25/shipping_tips)
