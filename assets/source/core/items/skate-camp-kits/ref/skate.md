# Skate obstacles

- The timber kicker follows the low wedge and plywood construction of the OC Ramps GB Kicker. The game ramp has a rubber transition strip and exposed timber framing below the deck. [OC Ramps GB Kicker](https://ocramps.com/products/gb-kicker)
- The grind rail takes its continuous flat bar and two upright supports from the Freshpark Ultimate Grind Rail Kit. Narrow rubber shoes stay inside the post footprint. [Freshpark Ultimate Grind Rail Kit](https://freshpark.com/products/ultimate-grind-rail-kit-by-freshpark)
- The ledge takes its long flat deck and straight edges from the OC Ramps 4 Foot Grind Box. A stone cap over the timber base follows the cantilevered ledge in Grindline's Issaquah Skatepark plans. [OC Ramps 4 Foot Grind Box](https://ocramps.com/products/grind-box-4-foot), [Issaquah Skatepark plans, sheet SP3.1](https://products.issaquahwa.gov/ActiveProjects/ASDP16-00006/ASDP16-00006%20Notice%20of%20Decision.pdf)

The geometry follows the collision frames in `content/skate-park.ts`. The kicker has a 1.2 m wide deck rising 0.5 m along 1.6 m. The rail has a continuous 3 m by 5 cm top at 0.225 m and two posts. The ledge has a 2.4 m by 0.6 m top at 0.2 m. The top planes and their edges stay in the same place at each level. Side framing, tapers and bevels fit within the shapes.

The models were built for SNARK. The linked products and drawings informed construction and shape. All surfaces use the game's repeating wood, iron, limestone and rubber textures, with matching flat colours at levels 2 and 3.
