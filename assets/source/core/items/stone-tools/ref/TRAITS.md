# The stone tools: trait sheet

What makes a stone pick, a stone hatchet and a spear read as ARK: Survival Evolved's first tools, written for the
concept painter, the modeller and the material maker. It describes the tools as players know them, from public pages
only (see [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md)). Ratios are by eye; treat every number as "about".

## What the three share

1. **They are made from what a survivor picks up in the first minute.** A stick, a rock, some plant fibre. Nothing is
   sawn, nailed or forged. The recipes say it: the pick is 1 stone, 1 wood and 10 thatch; the hatchet 1 flint, 1 wood and
   10 thatch; the spear 2 flint, 8 wood and 12 fibre (ark.wiki.gg).
2. **Three materials, always the same three.** A pale, unpainted wooden haft; a binding of tan plant fibre wound round
   the joint; a grey knapped stone or a darker flint head. Nothing else is on them: no grip wrap, no paint, no feathers,
   no metal.
3. **The binding is the visual centre.** On all three the head is held on by a thick wad of wound fibre that is wider
   than the haft: it is what says "primitive" more than the stone does.
4. **Dull colours.** The primitive tier is browns, tans and greys. Every later tier (metal, then the coloured ones)
   reads by contrast with this.
5. **One-handed, held low, swung.** The pick and hatchet are short tools a little over a forearm long; the spear is
   about a survivor's height and is held about a third of the way up.

## Each by its silhouette, most important first

| Tool | The read at a glance | Proportions, by eye |
|---|---|---|
| Stone pick | A T or a hammer: a short straight haft topped by one big rock lashed across it, pointed, sticking out mostly to one side. It is the heaviest-looking of the three | Haft about 0.5 m. Head across the haft about 0.2 to 0.25 m, about half the haft's length; deep and blocky, not a thin blade |
| Stone hatchet | A flag: a short haft with a flat, dark, wedge-shaped flint blade lashed to one side near the top, its edge facing out | Haft about 0.5 m. Blade about 0.1 to 0.15 m out from the haft and about as tall along it; thin edge-on |
| Spear | A long line with an arrowhead: a long thin pole with a pale-grey or dark leaf of stone lashed to its tip | About 1.8 to 2 m long; point about a tenth of that; pole thin, about 2.5 cm |

The pick and the hatchet must not be confused: the pick is a thick grey lump that points; the hatchet is a thin dark
sheet that cuts. Seen edge-on (as a survivor looks down a tool in first person) the pick head is a fat block and the
hatchet a sliver.

## What does not matter to the read

The exact shape of the flake scars, the wood species, the number of turns of binding, how the head is fixed under the
binding, the knots on the haft. Those are ours to design.

## What is deliberately ours

SNARK is a parody with its own look: readable, low-poly, chunky and a little playful. Familiar at a glance, ours on a
second look.

- **Chunky.** Heads about 15% fatter than a real tool's, so they read in first person and as a small icon. Big,
  flat knapping facets in the geometry itself, the low-poly look, with the fine ripples of the stone in the texture.
- **The haft is a branch, not a dowel.** A slight kink, a sawn-off twig stub, a flared butt where it was broken off the
  tree, bark left in a patch or two. Stripped wood worn pale and smooth where the hand goes.
- **A figure-eight binding with two loose tails.** The fibre crosses over the head in an X and winds round the haft
  above and below it; two cut ends stick out of the knot. On the spear the tails are a short tuft.
- **A pale fresh edge.** Where a flint is knapped its edge shows lighter than its face. The hatchet's and the spear's
  cutting edges carry a pale band; the pick's point is worn pale at the tip. It is the one bright accent on each tool.
- **The pick's point curves a little,** like a beak: a little character in the most common tool in the game.
- **Grey stone for the pick, dark flint for the hatchet and spear,** as the recipes have it: the pick is stone, the
  others flint.
