## One design change: the hatchet's blade

Make it a knapped flint: a little asymmetric, its outline rounder and slightly lopsided (one shoulder fuller than the other, the edge a smooth convex curve that sweeps a little further forward at its lower end), with two or three shallow notches where flakes came off the sides. Keep its sizes (edge 0.14 m along z at y = +0.115, 0.08 m at the haft, 24 mm thick at the root) and keep the pale edge band and the cortex on the back.
