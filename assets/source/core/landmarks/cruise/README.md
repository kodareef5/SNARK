# Icon of the Seas reconstruction

This full-scale moored reconstruction uses an original modular model, fitted public interiors and ordinary native walking. It includes the Royal Promenade and Pearl through-stair, Central Park, Surfside, AquaDome theater, Chill Island, Thrill Island, the Hideaway, Grove terraces, dining/theater/ice arena, bridge lookouts and medical/service rooms. Interior, ocean-facing, balcony and upper-suite examples are playable rooms; the repeated accommodation facade cells are explicitly not advertised as individual rooms. Six decorative slide courses, eighteen enclosed lifeboats, working circulation guards and restrained wayfinding complete the exterior identity. Native production image and capacity evidence accompanies final acceptance; source completeness alone does not certify frame cost.

The builder supplies 365 m overall length, 48.5 m waterline beam, 57.8 m deckhouse width and 65.8 m bridge width. Actual photographs and the frozen Profile2250 visitor plan guide the reconstruction. Floor elevations, hull cross-sections, provisional −9.3 m keel and 75 m funnel-top height are explicitly inferred. The bridge, hull and deckhouse carry independent measurement tags. See [reference provenance](ref/REFERENCES.md) and the [shared metre contract](ref/LAYOUT.md).

`author_layout.py`, `layout_geometry.py`, `layout_production.py` and `layout_edges.py` generate the single JSON authority. Prototypes own bounded collision and visual geometry; instances reuse those definitions at stable transforms. Source parts remain within ±16 m per axis and a conservative 14.5 m horizontal radius, preserving native navigation margin. Tapered decks use true polygon clipping, including triangular edge prisms. Rooms expose occupied footprints and holes where their plans follow curved decks or open neighborhoods. `model.py` and the interior, lifeboat and AquaTheater helpers generate six native levels with the closed-solid primitives in `parts.py`. Near-flat levels preserve windows, doors, stairs and landings. Glazed areas use clear framed apertures and solid window collision; they do not rely on unsupported transparency. Pools have real shallow basins, exit steps and decorative contained surfaces. The occupied pool-service lounge retains 2.66 m beneath the actual basin slab. No upper-deck pool creates map water.

The architect publishes AquaDome's 57 m width and 17 m height. Its inferred 70 m longitudinal extent and center at Z112 follow the actual broadside photograph, keeping the crown low and well behind the stem. Pearl retains the builder's 15 m diameter; the architect's conflicting 53 ft statement is documented rather than silently substituted. Lifeboats use the published Palfinger MPC49 family envelope of 15×5.5×4.2 m, with original fittings guided by actual photographs; see [lifeboat sources](ref/LIFEBOATS.md). The vessel is static. Slides, performance machinery and pools are decorative, and interiors are original fitted interpretations rather than authenticated construction plans.

The enclosure, hull-detail and entry helpers add supported tier walls, inward cabin fronts, sealed porthole rhythm and actual route-crossing metadata. Bounded 20×20 m floor groups retain each clipped void, with X/Z origins snapped before local geometry is derived. Cardinal guards use 1/2/4/8 m panels wholly inside the original protected run; a final overlapping panel retains coverage without moving an opening. Straight diagonal and sloped rails keep their authored frames. The small guard/balcony tubes use eight sides at the finest level and six at the next, preserving their radius and posts. Contiguous slide runs share bounded modules. The Pearl uses continuous indexed inner and outer sector surfaces, capped only at sector and portal boundaries. Touching closed primitives remain independent mesh islands so internal caps cannot reverse adjacent visible faces during normal recalculation.

`tex/paint.py` authors the original marine enamel, wood, textile, tile, metal and wayfinding atlases. Reference pixels are never sampled. Fine deck surfaces repeat the finish within 4 m spans, including tapered fragments. Intentional semantic materials provide native flat colours; actual Principled base colours preserve fallback appearance. Far proxies also use representative per-kind colours, including pale/blue funnel bands, dark accommodation glazing, yellow lifeboats and six colored slide courses. `build.ts` keeps native measured surface errors and adds the accepted cumulative 8 mm appearance-loss floor for nearby finishes. The shared texture budget remains 64 MiB of decoded RGBA mip data.
