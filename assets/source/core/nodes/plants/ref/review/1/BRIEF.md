# A second opinion on the low plants

You painted the concepts and atlases for five plants of a survival game's undergrowth (berry bush, saw palmetto, fern, sea grape, creeper). They are now built as models from your atlases, and the pictures attached and in this folder are those models drawn by the game's own renderer: one sun, its sky, no shadows, on a plain checkered test ground (ignore the ground and the plain sky).

- `game_patch.png` and `game_inside.png`: a 20 m square of all five, from its edge and from inside it, on the medium setting (cut-out leaves with their textures).
- `game_row_<kind>.png`: every individual of one kind in front, and below them what is left of each when a player has harvested it (the bush stripped of its berries, the palmetto and fern cut back, the sea grape stripped, the creeper torn off its heap).
- `game_bush_four_sides.png`: one berry bush from four sides at 3 m.
- `game_patch_lowpoly.png`: the same patch on the low setting, which draws the plants without textures, in flat palette colours.

The concepts are in `../concept/` (start with `coast/undergrowth.png`, `berry/direction_a.png`, `ground/palmetto.png`, `ground/fern.png`, `coast/seagrape.png`, `coast/creeper.png`).

Look hard, as an art director who has to decide whether this ships, and write `CRITIQUE.md`:

1. For each kind, what is wrong with it against its concept and against the best foliage you know in games, most important first. Be concrete in terms a modeller can act on: how many leaf cards or fans or fronds, how big, how they are tilted and spread, the outline, the colour and value, what the harvested state should show. Say what is right too, briefly, so it is kept.
2. Whether a new player would find the berry bushes at a glance among the rest, and tell its berries by colour.
3. The low setting: does each plant still say what it is in flat colours?
4. The three changes that would do the most for the whole undergrowth.

Do not paint or change anything; write the critique only. Work only in this folder.
