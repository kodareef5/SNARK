# A fourth look at the low plants

Your third critique (`../round3/CRITIQUE.md`, pictures in `../round3/`) held the set on three points. The pictures here are the same views after a pass on just those, drawn the same way (one sun, its sky, no shadows, a plain test ground to ignore); the palmetto and sea grape did not change and are not shown.

What changed: the berry bush has fewer and smaller windows, its bunches stand further out from their sprays, and on low each bunch is three rounded lobes of berry colour; the creeper hangs twice as many strands from high on its shoulders in front of its shell, and the shell is drawn in at its foot behind them; the low fern is a narrow midrib with slim leaflets swept toward the tip on each side. The budgets (1600, 1600, 400 and 60 triangles a level) still hold.
