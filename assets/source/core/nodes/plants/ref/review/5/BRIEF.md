# A fifth look: the berry bush and the creeper

Your fourth critique (`../4/CRITIQUE.md`) held the set on the berry bush and the creeper; the coordinator has since accepted the fern, the palmetto, the sea grape and every harvested state, and agrees with you on the other two. These pictures are the two after another pass. The game now draws its medium, high and ultra settings lit: a sun, a sky table, exposure and a tone curve, as here; the low setting is still flat colour. What changed. The bush: every spray ends inside the outline, its twig thicker so it can be seen to carry it, the top ones rising out of the crown; sprays face up to the light and are lit as the round of the crown would be, so none turns dark at the rim; the outline is drawn in toward the stems at the foot; the leaves are rougher, so the sky no longer greys them; each berry is a lit sphere with a small white catchlight and a paler bloom on top, so near-black has a shape; on low, each berry cluster is a few round beads rather than one solid. The creeper is rebuilt: the rubble it climbs is under it all; over that the vine's own mass, a lumpy shell in deep shade that dips under the stone here and there; over that its leaves stand off in two layers, two rings of them round every shoulder and a ring round the whole outline reaching out and down past it; strands hang from the shoulders and runners of leaves creep out over the ground. Each plant still holds 1600, 1600, 400 and 60 triangles a level.
