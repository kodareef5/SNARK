# An eighth look: the berry bush, and the creeper on low

Your seventh critique (`../7/CRITIQUE.md`, pictures in `../7/`) passed the creeper on medium and held it on low for its broad panels, and held the bush for blades across its lower half and an empty middle seen through its largest opening. These are the same views after a pass on just those, drawn the same way (medium lit, low flat; ignore the test ground and sky), with `game_bush_black.png`.

What changed. The bush's inner sprays are now a second shell inside the first, by the same rule as the outer (each in the shell, running along it and facing out, spread evenly round it), so through a gap the eye meets leaves in shade, not bare spokes and the far side; the old inner sprays, which climbed from the branches and showed their edges from the side, are gone. On low, every leafy run on the creeper is two small leaf-shaped diamonds apart along its line, and the strands and rim runs a thin stem with small leaves; no run is drawn as a panel.
