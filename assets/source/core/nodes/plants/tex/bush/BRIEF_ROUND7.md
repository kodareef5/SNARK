# The berry bush atlas, round 7

Round 6's berries read as fruit in every colour. One change, in the packed layer only.

The game's lit tiers light a surface by its sky as well as its sun, and the sky's share of a highlight grows toward the edge of a surface turned away from the eye. At the leaves' present roughness (0.68 to 0.78), the bush's many sprays seen at a slant take a pale blue-grey film of sky over their green, and the whole bush reads washed and grey beside the fern and the sea grape. Drawn at 0.95, the same bush is green.

- **Leaf faces** (sprays, inner sprays, the stripped and fallen sprays, single leaves): roughness 0.88 to 0.94, keeping the small variation between leaves.
- Berries, catchlights, stalks, stems and bark as round 6 left them.
- Every other channel and layer unchanged.

Rebuild with `build_atlas.py`, run `check_atlas.py`, and add to `REPORT.md` what changed and the new leaf roughness mean.
