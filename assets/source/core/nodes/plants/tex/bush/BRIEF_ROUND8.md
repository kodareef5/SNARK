# The berry bush atlas, round 8

The reviewer finds every berry colour reads except near-black, which at about 7 m still sinks into the dark leaves: the bloom of round 6 is too small and too faint to survive being seen from that far, where a berry is a few pixels and its catchlight is averaged away. One change, inside region 0 only.

- **Broaden the bloom** on every ripe berry, on the sprays and in the bunches: a soft paler cap over the upper 40 to 50 percent of the berry, reaching about 150 to 170 at its brightest and falling smoothly to the body grey, so at a distance the berry's top half is clearly paler than its bottom. Keep the lower edge dark (55 to 65) and the body between as now. The ratios are what the dye keeps: near-black then shows a grey sheen over its top, red a lighter red, white stays white.
- Catchlights, calyx dots, unripe berries, leaves, stalks and every other channel and layer as round 7 left them.

Show the six dyes on the bunches and one spray against a dark leaf background as before, and beside them the same at a quarter size (as the game sees it at about 7 m), so the near-black read can be judged there.
