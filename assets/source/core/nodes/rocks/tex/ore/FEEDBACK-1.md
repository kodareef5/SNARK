Read `BRIEF.md` again, then your `REPORT.md`, then this. The build is careful and the checks are thorough, and the pictures are not yet rock. The concept's `NOTES.md` now exists in `../../ref/concept/`: read it, especially its colour table and "what the modeller must not miss", and look again at the lower left of `surfaces.png` there, which is the ore rock's skin at about a metre across.

- **stone reads as grey felt or plaster.** Seen at a metre it shows almost nothing: the middle of its height spans under a millimetre, and its colour barely moves. A hard dark rock at arm's length shows its grain and its breaks: individual mineral grains half a millimetre to two millimetres across, dark and light, some glinting; small hackly fracture facets from a few millimetres to a few centimetres across with crisp edges; hairline cracks; and mottling between grey-green and blue-black over 5 to 30 cm. Keep the mean colour, but let the darkest grains reach about 35 and the lightest specks about 120 in sRGB. Give the height 3 to 6 mm of real relief over most of the picture, facets with sharp steps, not a soft wash. "Quiet" in the brief means no busy pattern from far away, not no surface.
- **fresh** has the same trouble. A fresh break of this stone is crisp: conchoidal ripples and sharp steps, a lighter cooler grey-green, grains glittering. Stronger and crisper relief than the weathered skin.
- **vein reads as yellow sponge or painted plaster.** Massive sulphide ore is a packed mass of crystals: blocky and cubic grains from 2 to 15 mm with flat faces and sharp edges, each face a slightly different tone of brass, dark tarnish along the grain boundaries, angular patches of milky quartz between, a little bronze and iridescent tarnish here and there. The relief is those faces and steps, 2 to 5 mm. It should look like ore a geologist would pick up, from a metre.
- **rust cover is a barcode.** The trails are thin parallel lines of one width. Rust bleeding from a seam is a stain that starts broad and dense at its source, runs down in a few wider soft tongues with darker drip lines inside them, splits and fades, and pools a little where the surface steps out. Fewer, broader, more varied.
