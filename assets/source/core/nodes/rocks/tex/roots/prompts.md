## P01 — fig root bark albedo

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: a square, seamless game PBR BASE COLOR texture for living strangler fig ROOT BARK, showing a flat 75 cm by 75 cm area. A material swatch only, filling the entire square edge to edge.
Subject: smooth thin grey-brown to silver-grey bark of thick living subtropical strangler fig roots. Supple dense skin, broad quiet smooth areas, finely wandering shallow wrinkles, short slender crease groups, a few very shallow hairline cracks, scattered short lenticel dashes across the local grain, irregular pale rubbed/scuffed areas, a very small amount of muted grey-green lichen and restrained brown grime inside some creases. Beautiful fine photographic material detail. It must read as living wood skin, smooth and pliable.
Orientation: no single overall grain direction. Small local groups of fine wrinkles curve and wander in different directions across the image, balanced horizontal, diagonal and vertical orientations. No long parallel stripes, no big branching roots pictured, no trunk silhouette. The geometry will supply all root shapes. The overall surface stays smooth and continuous.
Colour: restrained warm silver-grey, pale grey-brown and taupe. Average about sRGB (146,134,116). Modest tonal variation, subtle pale scuffs. Very little green. No orange wood.
Lighting: perfectly flat, unlit diffuse albedo, orthographic straight-on material scan, fully cross-polarized. Absolutely no cast shadows, no directional illumination, no bright edge on one side of wrinkles, no specular highlights, no vignette or broad light/dark gradient. Dark fine crease pigment is okay.
Composition: continuous random natural surface, equal detail across the entire square, seamless repeating in X and Y with matching edges. No standout central feature.
Avoid: rugged oak bark, thick plate bark, deep fissures, dead split timber, tree rings, knots, branch outlines, stone pitting, sponge, cobblestone shapes, sharp flakes, large dark cavities, exposed rock, sand, leaves, moss clumps, objects, perspective, labels, borders, text, watermarks. Produce only the single square bark albedo texture.
```
