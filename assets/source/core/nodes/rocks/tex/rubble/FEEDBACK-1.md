# Feedback on round one

Read `BRIEF.md` again, then your `REPORT.md`, then this. The build is careful and the layers agree, and most of the pictures are too plain to be what they say. Look again at the lower right of `../../ref/concept/surfaces.png` (broken concrete with its aggregate standing out, the pink stucco face, the rebar), at `rubble.png`, and read `../../ref/concept/NOTES.md`. Each material is seen from a metre or two when a player quarries a heap, and from 10 m along a street: at a metre it must be unmistakably what it is.

- **broken** is the closest, and reads as polished terrazzo: flat, the stones cut level with the cement. A snapped edge of old concrete is rough: the aggregate stands out as rounded and broken pebbles from 5 to 25 mm, some plucked out and leaving sockets, the cement between crumbly and pitted. Give the relief that: pebbles proud, sockets deep, 10 to 20 mm of real height over the picture, and colour that follows it.
- **cast** is a plain grey sheet. Old cast concrete in a hot wet climate shows its making and its age: bug holes (small air voids, 1 to 8 mm, some in clusters), faint form-board lines or panel seams, fine crazing, a grain of sand and cement, rust-brown and black streaks of rain staining, patches of grey-green algae and dark grime, worn-through spots where the aggregate shows. Keep the mean in the colour table's concrete, but put that life in it.
- **stucco**: the render under the paint is a featureless cream. Real render has a trowelled or sprayed texture (a fine stipple or swirl), hairline crazing and a few larger cracks. The paint layer flakes evenly all over, like a camouflage print: make the flaking uneven, as weather leaves it, with large areas still whole, others mostly gone, flaking that clusters along cracks and in bands, with lifted curling edges at the borders, and the paint thicker and more chalky in the whole areas.
- **coral** is a fine cream speckle. Coral stone is visibly made of coral: in a sawn block you see sections through coral heads (rings and cups of polyps, meandering brain-coral grooves), broken shells, worm tubes, and open holes from a few millimetres to three centimetres, cream to buff with darker fillings. A player who has seen it should know it from concrete at two metres.
- **block** is a plain grey sheet. A cinder block face is open-textured and gritty: a coarse speckle of dark and light grains, many small voids, a few chipped spots, a little grime.
- **rust** is good.
- **Folder size.** The folder is 440 MB. Keep what rebuilds the delivery and what a reader needs (sources, prompts, scripts, the delivered maps, the `checks/` pictures named in the brief, the report); delete superseded iterations, per-material check folders the brief did not ask for, and intermediate arrays the build can make again.

Make broken, cast, stucco, coral and block again with this in mind, check them as the brief asks, and update `REPORT.md`.
