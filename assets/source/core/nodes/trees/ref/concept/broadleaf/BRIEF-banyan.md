# The banyan: concept art, round 2

You painted the strangler fig, the mangrove and the live oak of this game's ruined subtropical city (`DESIGN.md`, and the pictures in this folder). The game also has a fifth tree, which this round is for: **the banyan**. Work only in this folder. Do not run git commands that write. Do not look up, copy or feed any other game's pictures, or any photograph, to the image tool; this is our own design, painted from words. Your own pictures in this folder may be given to the image tool freely.

## What it is

A banyan is a fig of the same family as the strangler fig, with the same smooth pale bark and leathery dark leaves, that has grown old and wide: the aerial roots that hang from its great horizontal limbs have reached the ground, rooted, and thickened into **pillar roots**, secondary trunks that hold the limbs up, so one tree becomes a grove, a colonnade of grey columns under one broad dark roof. Between the pillars there are open halls of shade where a player walks; curtains of thin hanging roots that have not yet reached the ground; old paving lifted by the roots. In the ruined city a banyan has taken over a plaza or a roundabout, and one of them has grown a pillar through the middle of a fallen bus shelter or a rusted car.

It is the same game as before: a tree is harvested by striking a trunk with a stone hatchet, and a used-up one leaves a stump until it grows back. For a banyan the main trunk is struck; its pillars stand as solid posts a player walks round (the game gives each pillar its own collision post), and everything thinner than a wrist hangs free and is passed through.

Three individuals, all **14 to 18 m tall and 22 to 30 m across**, the main trunk 2 to 2.5 m across:

- **B1, the colonnade**: a main trunk and 6 to 9 pillar roots 0.3 to 0.7 m thick, spread under its limbs 4 to 10 m from the main trunk, so that from the side it is a row of columns under a long flat roof.
- **B2, the young banyan**: a main trunk and only 2 or 3 pillars, near the trunk, the first ones; many curtains of thin hanging roots that have not yet arrived; a rounder crown.
- **B3, the swallower**: pillars that have grown down round a rusted car or a bus shelter of the old city and lifted it, a broken limb, a lopsided crown.

Rules from the game: nothing solid lower than 2.3 m between the pillars except the pillars themselves; pillars are near-vertical posts, not arches; the crown is built of distinct clumps of leaves with sky between them, not a ball of noise.

## What to paint

The same look as your fig, oak and mangrove pictures: stylized realism at a finished game's best settings, warm afternoon sun.

1. **`banyan_lineup.png`**: B1, B2 and B3 side by side on an old plaza, a person (1.8 m) for scale, from the side far enough off to see each whole.
2. **`banyan_hall.png`**: a player's view at eye height inside B1, among its pillars and hanging roots, looking out to the bright plaza.
3. **`banyan_far.png`** and **`banyan_far_flat.png`**: a few banyans among the fig, oak and mangrove of `hammock_far.png` seen from about 400 m, painted and as flat-coloured low-poly volumes, so it is clear how a banyan reads apart from a fig at a distance (wider, flatter, a row of pale posts under it).
4. **`banyan_stump.png`**: what is left of B1 when the player has cut it down: the main trunk hacked off at about knee height, the pillars cut to stumps too.

## Deliver

The pictures as PNG in this folder, as large as the image tool gives. Add a **banyan** section to `DESIGN.md` in the style of the others: each individual's height, crown width, trunk and pillar diameters, where each pillar stands (distance and direction from the main trunk), how the limbs run out to carry the pillars, crown lobes and card groups, hanging roots, colours, and how it reads at 3 m and at 500 m. Keep everything you generate and do not keep in `source/`, and your exact prompts in `prompts.md`. Look hard at each picture and paint again until it is the best banyan you have seen in any game.
