# Live oak leaves, second set: round 3

Read `BRIEF.md`, `BRIEF_ROUND2.md` and your `REPORT.md` first. Round 2 is on the crowns in the game: `feedback/round2_game_30m.jpg` and `feedback/round2_game_6m.jpg`. Beside them, `feedback/concept_crown_detail.jpg` is the concept's crown at about the size the 30 m view draws it.

What round 2 got right and must keep: the colours (warm olive, 105/115/65 and 88/98/56), the roughness (0.95), the moss, the fern, the clean alpha and margins, the tiling of the mass.

What is wrong: **the mass reads as cobblestones or a turtle's shell**, a regular tiling of flat discs each ringed by a thin dark line, and the clusters carry the same cracks. On the crown that is a pattern printed on a ball, not leaves. Look at the concept detail for what a live oak's crown is made of at this distance:

- **Leaves larger than botany**: the concept paints them about 8 to 12 cm long and 4 to 6 cm wide, rounded ovals, so each one is visible from 10 m and a rosette from 30 m. Paint them at that size here, for every region.
- **Rosettes**: six to nine leaves radiating from each shoot tip, 30 to 50 cm across, their tips raised or lowered, never flat. The crown is rosettes over rosettes at different depths.
- **Overlap, not tiling**: a rosette's outer leaves lie over its neighbour's like shingles; sizes vary; no outline runs round any of them.
- **Shade is pooled, not drawn**: where the eye looks down between groups of rosettes into the crown, a darker hollow of deeper leaves, irregular, five to fifteen centimetres across, darker in base colour and in occlusion. The deeper a leaf lies, the darker and bluer it is; the leaves on top are the clusters' tone, and a few tips lighter and yellower.
- **Each leaf tilted**: leaves in a rosette lie at 20 to 50 degrees to the picture, each its own way, so under the game's sun the leaves facing it light up and the rest go dark, as in the concept. Put that tilt in the height (a 10 cm leaf tilted 40 degrees rises about 6 cm across its length; let the range grow to 120 to 160 mm) and the normal map follows from the height as it must.

The **clusters** are the same make: three to six of these rosettes overlapping into one clump, leaf tips making its outline, a few small gaps, no cracks. Keep cluster 3 as the longer edge spray.
