# Live oak leaves, round 6: brighter sun leaves

Read your `REPORT.md` and the briefs beside it first. Your sprays are now the leafy edge of every head of the live oak's crown in the game: a dozen of cells 0 to 3 radiate from each head's opaque core (its picture is `../oak_crown/`), and they are what reads as leaves at 20 to 80 m. Side by side with the concept (`../../ref/concept/broadleaf/oak_lineup.png`), the crown's structure now holds; its light does not. Measured on the game's own pictures at 10:00 with the sun behind the viewer, the brightest tenth of the crown's pixels is about (120, 128, 81) where the concept's is (144, 144, 87): the sunlit leaves are too dark and too grey to glow as the concept's do. The game tints these leaves warmer in its material (linear 0.96, 1.0, 0.72), so the colour you paint is warmed and a little darkened after you.

Make the **sun leaves** (cells 0 and 2) brighter and a little warmer, a mean near (150, 152, 76), their brightest leaf faces toward (185, 182, 92), keeping the fresh growth in cell 2 the brightest yellow-green. Lift the **shade leaves** (cells 1 and 3) a little, to a mean near (96, 108, 64), so a head's lit and shaded sprays stay one plant. Keep the leaves' value structure within each spray (lit faces over darker ones), which is what makes them read at a distance.

Change nothing else: shapes, alpha, layout, margins, the strips, the mass, heights, normals and roughness stay byte for byte where the pictures do not change. Rerun your checks with the new targets, update `REPORT.md`, and remove what you supersede.
