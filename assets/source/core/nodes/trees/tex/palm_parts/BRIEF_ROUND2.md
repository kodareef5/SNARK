# Palm parts, round 2

Your palm parts set is on the palms in the game now. The coconut crown's fibre, the nuts, the stalks and the hacked cut are good. Two regions need another pass:

- **crownshaft**: on the tree it reads as smooth plastic: one flat saturated green with almost nothing in the normal map, so seen from below at 10 m it is a green pipe. Look again at `../../ref/concept/palms/palm_parts.png` (ROYAL — CROWNSHAFT) and `royal_lineup.png`: the crownshaft is glossy, but it is made of sheaths: long smooth overlapping sheath edges running up it, each a faint raised lip with a thin darker line beside it; soft lighter and darker vertical streaks; a deeper, slightly bluer green low down where it meets the grey trunk and a lighter yellow-green toward the top; a few small dry tan patches and fine vertical striations. Keep it smooth and glossy (roughness 0.25 to 0.35), but give it the structure: relief of a millimetre or two for the sheath edges and streaks, colour that varies by 10 to 15% in value across it. Its left and right edges must still meet.
- **root**: it is laid on the thin roots at both palms' feet, where they read as orange pegs. A palm's roots are a dusty grey-brown (`#78674A` in `DESIGN.md`), darker and soil-caked toward the ground and slightly redder only where freshly broken; fine lengthwise fibres and small root hairs. Repaint the region in those colours, with its top row (where a root leaves the trunk) a little paler and greyer, and its bottom row (where it goes into the soil) dark with soil. Its left and right edges must still meet.

Everything else stays as it is: the layout, the other regions (do not change their pixels), the checks. Replace the four layers, keep the new sources and scripts, and add to `REPORT.md` what changed.
