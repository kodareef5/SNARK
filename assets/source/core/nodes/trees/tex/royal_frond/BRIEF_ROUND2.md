# Royal palm fronds, round 2

Your royal frond set is on the palms in the game now. The fronds' outlines and their feathery, many-ranked leaflets are right, the coverage holds as they shrink, and the dying frond is good. Two things fall short when a player looks up into a crown from below, which with the young royal palm is from 7 or 8 m:

- **The leaflets are flat strips.** In `checks/blade_detail.jpg` each leaflet is one even tone with a soft stroke, lit and unlit alike, so the crown reads as cut paper. A royal palm's leaflet is a narrow blade folded along its middle: one half lighter, one half darker, a fine pale midvein down the crease, a faint glossy sheen along it, the tip a little paler or browned, and real variation from leaflet to leaflet in width and tone, some overlapping others with a hint of shadow where they cross (that last in the occlusion, not the base). Put the crease in the height as well, so the normal map turns each leaflet's two halves to the light differently. Look at the coconut frond set in `../coconut_frond/` (`checks/detail.jpg`), which does this, and at the leaves in `../../ref/concept/palms/palm_parts.png`.
- **The edges are stair-stepped.** The alpha is hard, one pixel in or out, so a cut edge magnified is a staircase. Give the alpha a soft edge, rising from 0 to 1 over one to two pixels across every leaflet's outline, as an antialiased drawing would, so the game's filtering cuts a smooth line at one half. Keep the colour bled out under the alpha as you did.

Keep everything else as it is: the layout and outlines (the cards are cut to them, so do not let any frond grow wider), the three slots' characters, the 10 mm height range, the checks. Replace the four layers, keep the new sources and scripts, and add to `REPORT.md` what changed.
