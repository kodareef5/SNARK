# Egg shell colours

SNARK uses its existing species colours as a starting point and takes cues from living birds for the shell markings. The
patterns are original. Each shell uses the shared mask in the order ground, coarse blotches, fine freckles, wispy streaks,
short dashes and shoulder ring. Markings without a separate tint take the shell colour. The examples below describe which
markings should stand out in the 128 px inventory icons.

- **Dodo.** Warm ivory-grey with muted taupe dashes. The white rock pigeon egg inspired the restrained shell. [Cornell Lab of Ornithology](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Rock_Pigeon/lifehistory)
- **Parasaur.** Plain turquoise, taking the smooth wash from the blue-green American robin egg. [Cornell Lab of Ornithology](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/American_Robin/lifehistory)
- **Carbonemys.** Deep green with lifted sage freckles and soft moss dashes, after the pale specks on the dark emu shell. [National Gallery of Victoria](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/essay/fragile-beauty-emu-eggs-in-early-australian-gold-and-silverware/)
- **Trike.** Buff with broad umber blotches that stand on their own, inspired by the blotched Japanese quail egg. [Japan Poultry Science Association](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpsa1964/14/5/14_5_229/_article)
- **Raptor.** Slate blue with charcoal freckles, after the dark spots on the blue song thrush egg. [Reading Museum](https://collections.readingmuseum.org.uk/pdfs/Z115B.pdf)
- **Stego.** Olive with broad buff patches and short dark dashes. The olive, buff and brown come from common tern eggs. [Cornell Lab of Ornithology](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Common_Tern/lifehistory)
- **Pteranodon.** Honey-ochre with dark ink streaks, inspired by the pencil-like marks on common guillemot eggs. [British Birds](https://britishbirds.co.uk/journal/article/red-common-guillemot-egg-skomer)
- **Brachio.** Sand with muted pore-like freckles and pale grooves, after the cream shell and clustered pores of the ostrich. [Government Museum Chennai](https://govtmuseumchennai.org/uploads/topics/16528786805140.pdf), [ostrich eggshell pore study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27526124/)
- **Spino.** Cool slate with pale wisps and dark moss dashes. The grey and olive come from rock sandpiper eggs. [Cornell Lab of Ornithology](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Rock_Sandpiper/lifehistory)
- **Rex.** Rust brown with deeper brown blotches and softer freckles, inspired by the peregrine falcon's reddish-brown markings. [Cornell Lab of Ornithology](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Peregrine_Falcon/lifehistory)

The plain shoulder ring takes the ground tint. The fertile ring uses one chalk tint across all ten species, with the rest
of the shell matching the plain egg.
