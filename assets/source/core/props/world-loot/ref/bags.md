# Loot bag and death cache

- The loot bag takes its rounded base and gathered neck from a sailor's canvas ditty bag. The full belly slumps to one side and the mouth folds across the shoulder. [Canvas Ditty Bag, Intrepid Museum](https://intrepid.emuseum.com/objects/20684/canvas-ditty-bag)
- The death cache takes its stepped lid, paired latches and reinforced corners from molded equipment cases. A cloth carry grip rises above the lid. [1450 Protector Case, Pelican](https://business.pelican.com/us/en/product/Cases/Protector/1450)
