# Equipment trunk

The Pelican 0500 Protector transport case informed the broad cap and shoulder, the
reinforced clipped corners, and the carry wells at the ends. The trunk uses a dark
rim and bronze draw latches to keep the lid and closure visible at game distance.
[Pelican 0500 product sheet](https://business.pelican.com/us/en/product/Cases/0500/pdf)

The mesh uses the instrument pack's iron, rubber, and bronze material tiles.
