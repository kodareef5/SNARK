# Heap and flying pebble

- The heap takes its broad foot, uneven stacked lobes and off-centre folded bulge from the formed cattle pat in the grade 4 reference. The wider base stays at ground level and the warmer brown wraps over one side of the fold. [University of Queensland cattle assessment, page 4](https://research-support.uq.edu.au/files/61442/Score%20Sheet_Cattle%20-%2017_12_2021.pdf#page=4)
- The flying pebble takes its flattened oval body and rounded shoulders from the crinoid limestone pebble from Fife. One end tapers more than the other. A cooler mineral patch wraps across its flank. [Scottish Geology Trust beach pebble guide, page 10](https://www.scottishgeologytrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Beach-Pebble-Guide-Scottish-Geology-Trust2021v1-1.pdf#page=10)
