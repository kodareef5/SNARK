# Miami coastal building kit

The apartment puts shade **behind** a continuous balcony front. The shop puts glass **below** a continuous sign band. The garage leaves air **between** its decks. Preserve those three relationships when substituting finishes or selecting damaged pieces.

## Setting out

All sizes are **width × height × depth**, in metres. In the street elevation, **x** runs right from axis A. Axes A through E are at 0, 8, 16, 24 and 32. **u** starts at the left axis of the current bay, **v** at the left edge of a 4 m half-bay. **y** is depth measured from the front column centreline, positive toward the road. A quoted *face* is the streetmost surface. **F** is the top of the current floor, **R** the top of the roof slab. Heights given as + offsets in a module row are relative to F, unless the row names R or grade.

Place floors at 0, 4, 8 … R inclusive. A slab at F fills F−0.40 to F. A column segment begins at F and ends at F+3.60 against the next soffit. Start the ground slab below grade in the same way. Holes prescribed in a wall module are air or fitted glazing. Construct the remaining face from piers, sill panels and heads. Union overlapping concrete at seats, panel corners and landings. Cull buried faces and emit each shared column once.

For the palettes, V is rounded display luma on a 0–100 scale. Swatches represent painted target values in the sheets. At distance, retain the two or three large value groups described for each kind. Put tide marks at grade, runoff below joints, and rust at exposed steel. Let daylight reach the back of openings. The bright sky and the darkest glass should still separate from concrete in shade.

### Grid and distance substitutions

Every storey entrance from the stair or ramp meets a walkable floor on the 4.00 m grid. All structural axes on the illustrated fronts stay 8.00 m apart. Half-bays, window centres and core widths use 4.00 m subdivisions. The largest outboard offset is the apartment nose at +2.00 m. The canopy spans 2.20 m in depth because its root is recessed 0.20 m.

The 0.20 m lip and 0.15 m lower return give the slab its game-scale silhouette. The 0.04 m bevel, drip groove, aggregate, shutter roll lines and real bar diameters are close-view details. At road distance, use a flat top with a painted sun edge, a dark underside stripe and a spall material. Cull individual bars at the far distance. Keep the full-depth broken end, hanging slab and matching column-head mass. The board marks, lites and weather stains can fade without changing any building's rhythm.
