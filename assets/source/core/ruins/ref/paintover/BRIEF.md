# Paint-over: the walk from a downtown corner to the water, as it should look

Two pictures in this folder are what a browser survival game draws today on its high setting, at 14:00, eye height,
in a real coastal city (Miami) rebuilt as ruins years after a storm surge: `corner-high.png` looks east down a
downtown street toward the bay; `shop-high.png` looks across that street at a two-storey block of shops with rooms
above. The four drawing sheets beside them (`shop.png`, `apartment.png`, `parking.png`, `slab-ends.png`) are your own
earlier measured elevations of the three building kinds this street should show and of the slab edge they share;
they are the target vocabulary.

What the game can do: buildings are assembled from box-like concrete pieces on a 4 m storey and 8 m bay grid, each
part in one flat palette colour lit by the sun and sky, with a tiling world texture on it; small kit pieces can be
modelled. The ground is a height field with painted colour; a curb, sidewalk and gutter would be new.

## Deliverables

- `corner-paintover.png` and `shop-paintover.png`: each a paint-over registered to the game picture (same size, same
  camera, same horizon and vanishing point, the same buildings standing where they stand), showing the same moment as
  it should look once the street and buildings are right: the street's shape (a curb with a lip catching the sun, a
  continuous sidewalk past the entrances, a gutter, a driveway cutting the curb, grass and sand starting at joints and
  low spots), and the buildings reading as their kinds by their bays, ground floors and rooflines, a failed bay
  traceable to its break (a slab end with bars, a column stump, the fallen slab below it). Keep the game's light and
  palette: no new weather, no people, no text.
- `NOTES.md`: for each picture, a numbered list of the ten changes that matter most, most visible first, each said in
  terms a builder of boxes and kit pieces can act on (what piece, where, how big, what colour or value), and which of
  them you would cut first if only three could be made.

## The bar

A good paint-over could be mistaken for a later picture of the same game, not a concept painting of a different one.
Bad: a different camera, new buildings, cinematic grading, detail finer than a few centimetres doing the work.
