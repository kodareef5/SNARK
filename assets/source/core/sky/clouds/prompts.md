## 01: Low cloud mass source

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: grayscale cloud column-density source for a real-time tropical game sky, square image.
Create a directly overhead, orthographic view of a large field of scattered fair-weather tropical trade-wind cumulus above a perfectly black, featureless ocean. This is a cloud optical-mass diagram with photographic cloud morphology, not a conventional lit sky photograph. Pure black empty space covers about 65 percent. About 35 irregular detached cloud heaps distributed nonuniformly across the whole square, in several loose clusters and short broken wind rows; a mix of large and medium clouds and tiny torn scraps, with open black channels between clusters. Main clouds 1/15 to 1/7 of image width. Round swelling cauliflower lobes join irregularly within each heap, finer billows at their margins, transparent wisps departing their edges. Avoid a single dominant cloud or a recognisable symbol.
Only grayscale density: white thick central masses, medium grey intermediate mass and thin soft grey fringes, black completely clear air. Every lobe is brightest at its local centre and falls off in every direction equally. No horizontal light direction at all, no shaded side, no cast shadows, no bright rim, no directional gradient. Density has intricate correlated structure at several scales, not static grain. Crisp naturally irregular detail in thick lobes, soft but structured dissolving boundaries. Clouds are seen strictly from above, never side profiles, no horizon, no blue, no ground, no sun, no text or borders. Fill the square as a texture; allow some clouds to continue beyond edges. 2048 square if possible.
```
