# Industrial process stations

- A flared feed hopper tapers into the grinder's horizontal mill drum. The exposed shaft end and cast motor identify the drive, while low rails hold the vessel over a discharge chute. [Fitzpatrick FitzMill D6A hammer mill](https://www.fitzpatrick-mpt.com/products/hammer-mills-m5a-d6a)
- The industrial forge uses a square insulated case, pale refractory around its dark front chamber and a raised rectangular flue cowl. The deep arched entrance takes its shape from an iron furnace casting arch. [L&L Special Furnace FW Series forge furnace](https://llfurnace.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/llfurnace-fw-g.pdf), [Beckley Furnace casting arch](https://beckleyfurnace.org/tour/furnace/)
- The industrial cooker uses a steam-jacketed kettle's curved bottom, rolled rim, domed lid and front draw-off valve. A low burner housing and side controls distribute the weight around the steel vessel. [Cleveland Range KGL-100 stationary steam-jacketed kettle](https://www.clevelandrange.com/product/kgl100-gas-steam-kettles-stationary/)

The meshes are original. The references supplied visual traits, and the textures carry fine wear and hardware.
