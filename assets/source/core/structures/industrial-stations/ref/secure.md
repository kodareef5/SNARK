# Secure industrial stations

The stations use original geometry based on exterior shapes.

- The vault takes its thick square door, exposed hinge barrels and central wheel from a Mosler bank vault door. The game version adds a low skid and a separate brass dial for a readable front. [Mosler Safe Company vault door, Monthly Bank News, October 1953](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/monthlybanknews/monthlybanknews_195310.pdf)
- The chemistry bench pairs the steel worktop and offset cabinet of Kewaunee lab furniture with the round belly and narrow neck of a period Pyrex flask. The bench carries solid, closed glass shapes. [Kewaunee steel laboratory furniture](https://kewaunee.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/steel-laboratory-furniture.pdf) and [Pyrex glass flask, Science Museum Group](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co8630866/glass-flask)
- The soul vault takes its cylindrical neck and raised controls from the CryoPlus storage cabinet. It uses a tall enamel belly, a stepped cap and a front-facing power panel. The panel and the strip below the cap share the on/off finish. [CryoPlus storage systems, Thermo Scientific](https://www.thermofisher.com/order/catalog/product/7405)
