# Utility appliances

These references guided the exterior shapes. The models use original geometry and shared station materials.

- The Honda EB5000X has a welded tubular frame, a top-mounted tank and a front panel set above the exposed engine. The SNARK generator pairs two hoops with fixed skids and leaves a clear view of the engine and alternator between the bars. [Honda EB5000X generator, owner’s manual](https://cdn.powerequipment.honda.com/pe/pdf/manuals/31zc2602.pdf)
- The Frigidaire DT 44A has a curved enameled door with a prominent latch. The SNARK refrigerator stretches that single-door profile to a full-height cabinet, with a pale enamel finish, a dark gasket and a metal pull. [Frigidaire DT 44A, circa 1952, Science Museum Group](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co8011602/frigidaire-electric-refrigerator)
- The Daikin Fit cabinet puts its circular fan behind a broad front grille and draws air through dark coil sections on its sides. The SNARK unit uses a recessed fan with a raised shroud and rectangular louvers in the side and rear panels. [Daikin Fit, side-discharge outdoor heat pump](https://daikincomfort.com/docs/default-source/dvfec/pb-fithp.pdf)
