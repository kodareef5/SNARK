Round three. The tier was looked at again against the best building art in games, and the verdict on all of it was: careful work that looks like a pattern printed on a box. The answer is mostly in the geometry, which has changed, and your picture has to change with it.

The new `layout/` has it, made from the same rows as before: `cells.png` (each flag's footprint in its own colour, black is mortar), `faces.png` (the face inside the bevel full colour, the bevel band half), `layout.json` (every flag's footprint and face ring in metres and pixels, and its rise at each corner) and `tiled.png`.

Paint to these outlines exactly: packed grit and mortar outside the footprints, the worn and chipped arris across each bevel band, each flag's own face inside its ring. Do not paint the bevel's shading into the base colour or normal; the geometry gives that. Paint what is smaller than it can: wear, pits, fossils, bedding, hairline cracks, grime. The height should still read as flags if laid flat: 16 mm black to white, mortar near black, faces about 10 mm.
