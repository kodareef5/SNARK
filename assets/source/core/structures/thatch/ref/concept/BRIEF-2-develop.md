Read `BRIEF-1-directions.md` and `DIRECTIONS.md` in this folder first: they hold the job, the rules every piece obeys and what was painted in round 1. The art director has chosen **direction A, the long skirts**, as painted in `round1/a03_material_finish.png` (the strongest), `round1/a02_refined.png` and `round1/a_inside01.png`. Develop it now into the full set of pictures a 3D artist builds from. Work only in this folder, take as long as it needs, push every picture until it is something you would show proudly, and use your own accepted pictures as references for the next ones (your image tool can edit and extend its own pictures), so every view shows the same house built the same way.

## What to keep from a03

Two tiers of long reed hung on each wall, each tier a row of broom-like bundles tied at the top and flaring to pale, sun-bleached, ragged tips; the upper tier's skirt hangs well over the lower tier at mid-height and throws a deep broken shadow; a thin crooked rod lashed across each tier near its top; proud hand-cut posts at the corners and where two walls meet, lashed with pale cord; the shaggy eave with round joist ends showing under it; the low log-and-stake crib of the foundation with packed earth showing between; the doorway of rough jambs and an overhanging lintel; the light door of thin poles with a diagonal brace and vertical reed infill, hung on cord loops.

## What to change or fix

- **Size and shape exactly as the rules say.** The standard house is a 6 m square (two foundations by two), walls 3 m tall, so it is about as wide as it is deep, and only a little wider than it is tall. Round 1 painted houses longer than that. Each 3 m wall is one panel between two posts, so each side of the house shows exactly two panels and three posts (corner, middle, corner).
- **The roof is flat on top.** No slope. It is four 3 m square ceiling slabs laid edge to edge, 25 cm thick, whose top is level with the top of the walls. Its edge hangs out over the walls by up to about 30 cm and droops: a thick, ragged, sun-bleached eave all round, with rounded corners, joist ends showing under it.
- **One doorway**, in the middle of a panel: an opening 1.4 m wide and 2.3 m tall, its sill at floor level. The door is exactly that size and hangs on the left side as seen from outside, opening outward by a quarter turn.
- Bundles differ in width, fullness and length; the skirt's foot is ragged, not a straight hem; strays, hollows, bleached tips, sag between ties. No ruler-straight lines anywhere.
