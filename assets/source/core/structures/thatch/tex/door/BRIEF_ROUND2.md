# Door, round 2: warmer and richer

Round 1 is accepted in its layout, its alpha, its inside cord rows (keep them where they are: the model now has battens over them) and its build. Two changes:

- **Colour.** Beside the walls, which are now a warm straw gold (mean about (191, 146, 82)), the door reads pale cream (mean about (198, 168, 114)). Make it the same family as the walls, a touch lighter and more even since it is tighter work: the mean about (195, 155, 92), with sun-bleached reeds here and there and a little darker toward the bottom where hands and feet wear it. Not orange.
- **Life.** Reeds you can see one by one at arm's length: each round, with a light and a dark side and the odd node; bundles a little different in tone and tightness; the odd reed out of line. Put more of the relief in the height (about 8 to 10 mm visible), so the reeds read under a low sun.
