# Roof, round 2: sent back

Your first round in this folder is well made and checked, and it is not good enough. Put `roof_base.png` beside `../../ref/concept/house_high.png` and the difference is plain: the concept's roof is a rich, sun-warmed mattress of golden reed that you want to touch; this picture is a pale beige pattern of thin lines, like a vector drawing of reeds. The checks pass because they measure what the engine needs, not whether it is beautiful. This round is about that.

Keep the layout exactly as delivered (the course rows, the binding rows, the panel seam on the edges, the 3 m span, both wraps) and keep your build and check scripts as the way the set is made. Change the picture:

- **Colour.** Golden straw, the colour of the concept's roof in late sun, a little more weathered than the walls: base colour mean somewhere about (180, 145, 90), not (191, 172, 135). Variation that a hand-laid roof has: handfuls of reed a little lighter or darker, warmer or greyer, the odd darker damp patch and the odd bleached one. Do not compress the generated detail toward one flat wheat colour; keep its contrast.
- **Reeds.** Real reeds, a few pixels wide, each with its own light and dark side, laid in handfuls 5 to 10 cm across that lie at slightly different angles and pack against each other. The odd stray reed crossing its neighbours. It should hold up at 1:1 the way the wood plank example does.
- **Courses.** Each course line is where a fresh row of reed butts starts: a visible row of cut reed ends, chunky, with the course above lying over them and casting a shallow shadow line in the occlusion. It should read clearly from above at a distance as rows, as `house_high.png` reads.
- **Bindings.** Twisted cord a few millimetres thick, pulled tight so the reeds dimple and bulge between stitches, the stitches every 10 to 15 cm. Not a thin painted line.
- **Height.** Use more of it: reeds' roundness, the handfuls' bulge, the butt rows' lip and the bindings' pull. About 20 to 25 mm full range.

Generate more source pictures if you need them; reeds photographed close in raking light make the best material. Then deliver the same four maps, update your checks (add a side-by-side of your roof preview with `house_high.png`, and a 1:1 crop beside the wood example's), and update `REPORT.md`. Keep round 1's sources beside the new ones.

Do not use or imitate pictures from any existing game, and do not search for or download reference images. The concept pictures in this repository are ours.
