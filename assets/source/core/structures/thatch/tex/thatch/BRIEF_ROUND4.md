# Thatch wall, round 4: warmer tips, a longer fade

Round 3 is accepted: the layout, the bundle edges, the ties, the alpha and the build are right, and on the model the skirts hang as real ragged reed. In the house renders under a low warm sun one thing stands out: the tips. Every skirt ends in a band of near-white cream, sharply set off from the gold above it, so from ten metres each wall wears two white lace hems. In the concept (`../../ref/concept/house_hero.png`, `piece_wall.png`, `details.png` upper left) the tips are paler than the bodies but still straw: warm cream to pale gold, and the change from body to tip is gradual.

- **Tip colour.** The bleached ends about (214, 184, 132), warm, never grey-white, with some tips that stay golden and a few that are paler; in shade they must still read as straw.
- **A longer fade.** Bleaching starts about halfway down each skirt and grows toward the ends, bundle by bundle a little differently, so there is no line where it begins.
- **The bodies** a little richer: keep their mean about (191, 146, 82) but with more difference bundle to bundle and stronger darks deep between reeds.
