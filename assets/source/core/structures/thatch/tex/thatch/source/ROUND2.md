# Round 2 source record

The new appearance is generated by `../build_material.py` from the original two
image sources and `round2_palette.json`. No new source image was generated or
downloaded. The original images and `PROMPTS.md` remain unchanged here.

References inspected: our repository's `../../ref/concept/house_hero.png`,
`piece_wall.png`, and `DESIGN.md` (paths relative to the material root). The
user's round-2 RGB targets take precedence over the older design-sheet palette.
The concept pictures inform appearance and the comparison sheet; their pixels
are never sampled into the material.

`round1/` is a complete snapshot of the previous four maps, scripts, report,
requirements, guides, sources, masters, relief arrays, manifest and checks.
`round1_archive_sha256.json` records all 38 archived files. Keep the archive
frozen: the current checker uses its masks and construction data as regression
references and re-renders its maps under the same light as round 2.

The original geometry random stream remains seed 912731 (+1000 for the lower
tier). Appearance uses an independent stream, seed 936731 (+1000 for the lower
tier). It changes sheaf age/colour, individual stem pigments and roughness.
Every original stem path, radius, end, node, stray and cutout operation is
retained. Compression and cord impressions change local height only near the
two existing rod rows. Height still maps the full 0–16 mm range.

New `upper_master.png`, `lower_master.png`, `relief_layers.npz` and
`build_manifest.json` are reproducible intermediates. The manifest records
the palette, geometry and appearance seeds, per-sheaf RGB offsets, and counts
of dark and waxy stems. Transparent-pixel RGB retains the inherited treatment;
there are no new requirements or checks for it.

Rebuild from the material root with `python3 build_material.py`. Regenerate
evidence with `python3 check_material.py`, or run
`python3 check_material.py --rebuild` to also check byte-identical reproduction.
