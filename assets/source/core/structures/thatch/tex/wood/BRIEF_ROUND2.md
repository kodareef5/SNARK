The geometry wraps it round poles as short bands, with the picture's horizontal direction along the pole, so draw the turns **unrolled**:

- each turn of cord runs straight down the band, from its top edge to its bottom edge, and continues across the band's top and bottom edges as one turn would continue round a pole, so the band wraps top to bottom on itself;
- turns lie side by side along the band, touching, about 8 to 10 mm thick (11 to 14 pixels), each a two-ply twist whose plies are clearly visible as diagonal ridges;
