# Selection and visual review

All 21 image-tool outputs were inspected when generated. Selected: fourteen paintings. An additional original orthographic dimension guide is drawn from the specified envelopes, not from the paintings.

## Accepted changes

- Homestead front v03: preserves the thin flat roof and wide stair beside, rather than inside, the porch. The early two paintings have an overly narrow stair and exaggerated roof; they remain archived.
- Homestead overhead v01: matches the front view's porch, pillars, front openings and right-side stair; makes the rear right window visible. Roof is a continuous board floor. The central tile seam is intentionally subdued.
- Homestead field v01: house occupies a small part of the scene; the dark porch, light wood and flat roof remain legible.
- Wall v02: changed the initially repetitive course heights and reduced signature seams. Differing courses across the central post are the important improvement.
- Foundation v01: distinct deep perimeter sills, flush floor, credible end grain and structural corner dogs.
- Windowframe v01: clear unglazed opening, appropriate sill position and both-sided construction language.
- Doorframe v04: selected after height and width correction attempts. Painting still suggests a slightly narrower aperture than specified; the exact orthographic guide and 1400 × 2310 opening in DESIGN.md take priority.
- Door shut/open v01: left hinge from outside, rear brace correctly rises from lower hinge to upper latch. Open study is seen from the reverse. Hardware stand-off is visually exaggerated: model the recessed pull and 80 mm depth budget in DESIGN.md.
- Ceiling above/below v01: one walkable top, exposed underside, four internal joists in the selected underside painting. Four supersedes the early prompt's five.
- Pillar v01: integral shoulder and foot, visible end grain, contained head/foot design. Enforce 500 square rather than measuring perspective enlargement.
- Stairs v02: ten tread surfaces, enclosed side, no top guard. Rejected v01 grew an unwanted miniature wall over the top tread.
- Details v01: eight distinct views cover every requested connection, including separate head and foot studies.

## Modelling interpretation

These are painted design references, not calibrated CAD. The most consequential limits are supplied independently in dimensions.png and dimensions.json: opening sizes/locations, slab thicknesses, fixed step count and grid envelopes. All visible wear is subordinate to those limits. The upper wall/ceiling joint uses a shared housed zone; do not stack the ceiling above the wall. No picture is permission to add bigger capitals, wide sill overhangs or thicker iron.

The 200 mm double-sided wall skins, small corner reveal, rebated pull and hidden ceiling seat remain details to interpret using the written sections. The paintings communicate the desired exterior craft rather than exposing every concealed cut.

## Provenance

No web search or other-game image reference was used. Every image-tool input was an original image made in this folder's workflow. The supplied current renders were viewed only as the starting proportions/design problem. No git commands were run. Final selected paintings are copied byte-for-byte from native image-tool PNGs; no crop, resize, filter or retouch was applied outside the image tool.
