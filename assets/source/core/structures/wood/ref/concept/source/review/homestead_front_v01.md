# First painting review

Keep: attractive honey/umber hierarchy, sculptural tool-marked edges, proper dark openings, porch atmosphere, thick fitted deck boards.

Revise: roof fascia and exposed ends imply more than the allowed 250 mm slab; stairs have too many treads and are narrower than the required 3 m; stair starting position appears to invade the porch. Post capitals appear wider than the 500 mm envelope. Need a geometric guide to hold the six roof modules and side stair placement steady across all views. The next painting should preserve this material direction while correcting the envelopes.
