# The utility motorboat

An original four-seat open working boat, with a 6.8 m hard V hull and 2.6 m beam, a continuous keel and chines, rubber rub rail, raised gunwales, four independent upholstered seats, bridged low helm, clear two-pane windscreen, tapered bow cargo locker, one aft rigging locker and one articulated outboard. The stern-left recovery ladder is offset from the engine and propeller. It has no weapons or mounted weapon fittings.

The mechanical contract is `content/vehicles/boat-art.ts`. Coordinates are metres, +Y up, +Z forward, +X the pilot's left. The origin is on the keel plane. Y 0.60 is the rated-load waterline; buoyancy determines the lighter hull's resting height. The dry deck is at 0.73. Hull sides rise from 1.36 aft to 1.55 at the bow. The source loft has a real open U section: outer keel/chine/topsides join the gunwale, flared inner wall and deck, with no invisible solid box around the occupants.

The seat pelvis anchors are X ±0.48, Y 1.28, front Z 0.40 and rear Z −1.20. The generous 1.60 m row spacing gives the largest armored boots space behind the front seats. The helm's centre is X 0.48, Y 1.64, Z 0.67, with 0.18 m radius and ±0.65 radian steering. Its positions came from actual smallest and mixed-proportion survivor reaches before modelling. The dashboard bridges the footwells, with narrow centre and outer supports. The throttle pedestal was moved 80 mm outboard after the real arm clearance audit found an unnecessarily tight gap.

The five rigid bones are chassis, outboard, propeller, steering and throttle. The propeller is a child of the outboard; the shaft, cowling, cavitation plate and skeg follow steering as one connected assembly. Engine/exhaust/propeller sockets use outboard-local offsets; water contact, wake, spray, crew and boarding sockets remain on the chassis. The stern ladder's water approach, rungs and deck arrival occupy a corridor outside the propeller's swept area. The ladder-side stern corner stays open; its landing at X 0.83, Y 0.73, Z −2.70 has no locker or fitting in the foot corridor. The opposite rigging locker and scuppers remain inside the lower hull.

The original concept is design evidence. `rig.py`, `shape.py` and `model.py` are the editable source; the game's pack and renderer are the final presentation. Generic closed-part, UV layout and surface-writing machinery is reused from the compact/helicopter pipeline. Hull geometry, fitting layout, engine, livery and surface painting are original. Reproducible texel bakes and model-space painting keep base, normal, packed, dye, hurt and decay maps aligned. The hull's anti-fouling and stripe borders have semantic UV seams. These preserve the paint boundaries while the native surface simplifier reduces planar hull faces, before palette colours are baked for flat levels.

The windscreen has closed glass-edge pieces and metallic frames around a clear aperture. This is intentional: the low-poly fallback does not use texture alpha, so a full glass plane would become an opaque wall. The centre remains clear at every native level and before textures arrive. The two original static helm graphics are a coastal chart and engine-status face; actual vehicle telemetry is supplied by the live game HUD.

Civilian, rescue and police liveries share all geometry and mechanical/interior maps. Their original paint and lettering use three dye regions: paint, stripe and upholstery. The wreck deforms the same closed surfaces and uses completed damaged/decayed materials. No manufacturer geometry, photographed texture, real agency mark or livery is copied.

The exact-human audit skins every native body, armor, hair and beard mesh for both sexes in all four seats. Independent per-bone scale intervals cover continuous body, leg and passenger-arm proportions. Twenty-five head-look grid points plus an angular motion allowance bound continuous look. The nonlinear helm solver preserves bone lengths and pelvis position and mirrors the two grasp orientations; it is checked at every combined arm/torso proportion extreme for three wheel angles, plus 1,024 interior proportion samples at seventeen angles. This is sampled evidence for the nonlinear control solve, not an interval proof between every arm slider value.

The final full report tests 133,121,840 geometry points and 41,984 pilot profiles against the hull/deck, other seats, dashboard, supports, instruments and throttle pedestal. Minimum clearance is 87.7 mm; head clearance is 129.9 mm after the conservative continuous-look allowance. The greatest grip residual is 0.000000225 m. Native occupied reviews include large/small real armored bodies, forward pilot eyes, controls and both steering extremes.

Water foam is an original generated transparent sprite in the native cue pipeline. All three liveries reference one atlas cell. Bow spray uses the existing drop sprite. The runtime controls bounded emission from actual boat motion and water contact. Sound is assigned to the separate sound workstream; the art supplies engine and exhaust sockets.
