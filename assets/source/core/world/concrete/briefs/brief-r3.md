## Concrete

In a 3 by 3 tiling of the base colour, a few pale branching veins in the paste (Y-shaped, about 5 to 10 cm, the brightest near the top of the tile) are the one thing the eye finds again tile after tile. Soften their contrast and break them up so the paste's cloudy mottle carries no shape that recurs; keep the pinholes, sand and fine cracks as they are.
