## Mud

The wet hollows read as airbrushed dark smudges: soft grey-black blurs laid over the earth, with nothing of water in them, and at 3 by 3 the blobs are what the eye finds again. Make them fewer (about half), each a shallow puddle or wet film with a defined edge where the water stops (a thin darker meniscus, the crust lifting just outside it), fine silt and a few floating fibres visible through it, and the colour only a little darker than the mud around it: the game's sky reflection on their low roughness will carry the wetness.
