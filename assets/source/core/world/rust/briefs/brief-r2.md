## Rust

The base colour reads as camouflage: hard-edged blotches of orange and dark brown in an even pattern, with small grey flecks for metal. Corroded steel at arm's length shows layered rust: powdery orange oxide over darker, harder brown-black scale, raised blisters and flakes lifting with a dark undercut edge, pitting, streaky variation in the oxide's colour, a few islands of grey metal where a flake has just come away (rare, with crisp edges), and scattered remnants of old grey-green paint. Keep the mean (122, 78, 58), the even tile, and bare metal as metal.
