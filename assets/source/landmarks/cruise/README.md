# Icon of the Seas reconstruction

This full-scale moored reconstruction uses an original modular model, fitted public interiors and ordinary native walking. It includes the Royal Promenade and Pearl through-stair, Central Park, Surfside, AquaDome theater, Chill Island, Thrill Island, the Hideaway, Grove terraces, dining/theater/ice arena, bridge lookouts and medical/service rooms. Interior, ocean-facing, balcony and upper-suite examples are playable rooms; the repeated accommodation facade cells are explicitly not advertised as individual rooms. Six decorative slide courses, eighteen enclosed lifeboats, working circulation guards and restrained wayfinding complete the exterior identity. Native production image and capacity evidence accompanies final acceptance; source completeness alone does not certify frame cost.

The asset belongs to the map, under `assets/source/landmarks/cruise/`, with `cruise:*` content and mesh IDs. Logical prototypes and instances retain the original art and room provenance. `layout_cells.py` assigns actual 16 m XYZ visual cubes; `layout_collision_cells.py` clips their physical and far-look unions into those same cubes. Every physical module in a cube has the exact cell-center origin and yaw zero, and one real module draws the cell mesh. This keeps near/far chunk ownership coherent even when the landmark is moved or turned. Other modules use `mesh: "none"`; door states and three port signs remain independent. `collisionPrototypes`, `collisionInstances` and `collisionCells` are derived placement data, with source-part identities and a volume-conservation audit. Funnel collision now follows its original convex curved sectors, replacing the old invisible AABB corners. No phantom render-anchor collider is added. The landmark pack migration is in progress; historical core captures are not acceptance evidence for the new artifact.

The builder supplies 365 m overall length, 48.5 m waterline beam, 57.8 m deckhouse width and 65.8 m bridge width. Actual photographs and the frozen Profile2250 visitor plan guide the reconstruction. Floor elevations, hull cross-sections, provisional −9.3 m keel and 75 m funnel-top height are explicitly inferred. The bridge, hull and deckhouse carry independent measurement tags. See [reference provenance](ref/REFERENCES.md) and the [shared metre contract](ref/LAYOUT.md).

`author_layout.py`, `layout_geometry.py`, `layout_production.py` and `layout_edges.py` generate the single JSON authority. Prototypes own bounded collision and visual geometry; instances reuse those definitions at stable transforms. Source parts remain within ±16 m per axis and a conservative 14.5 m horizontal radius, preserving native navigation margin. Tapered decks use true polygon clipping, including triangular edge prisms. Rooms expose occupied footprints and holes where their plans follow curved decks or open neighborhoods. `model.py` and the interior, lifeboat and AquaTheater helpers generate six native levels with the closed-solid primitives in `parts.py`. Both textured near levels retain the actual circulation, rooms and clear framed public window apertures. Authored flat distant forms use dark exterior pane bands and omit hidden furnishings; the farthest forms retain sparse structural slabs and the outside silhouette. Pools have real shallow basins, exit steps and decorative contained surfaces. The occupied pool-service lounge retains 2.66 m beneath the actual basin slab. No upper-deck pool creates map water.

The architect publishes AquaDome's 57 m width and 17 m height. Its inferred 70 m longitudinal extent and center at Z112 follow the actual broadside photograph, keeping the crown low and well behind the stem. Pearl retains the builder's 15 m diameter; the architect's conflicting 53 ft statement is documented rather than silently substituted. Lifeboats use the published Palfinger MPC49 family envelope of 15×5.5×4.2 m, with original fittings guided by actual photographs; see [lifeboat sources](ref/LIFEBOATS.md). The vessel is static. Slides, performance machinery and pools are decorative, and interiors are original fitted interpretations rather than authenticated construction plans.

Facade tier batches retain separate pale and navy kinds and the exact near-visible frame boxes; public panes remain clear in both textured near levels; authored distant exterior panels use dark glazing bands. Plain wall batches preserve every original box and its individual wainscot. Hull batches join the original above/below-water pieces with separate near finishes, retaining a white far envelope above the sea. These source consolidations reduce submissions without removing the original surfaces or increasing renderer limits.

The enclosure, hull-detail and entry helpers add supported tier walls, inward cabin fronts, sealed porthole rhythm and actual route-crossing metadata. Bounded 20×20 m floor groups retain each clipped void. Adjacent levels share vertical modules of at most 16 convex parts and 30 m height; their full XYZ origins are snapped before local corners are derived. Each stack records its source decks and placement identities. Straight guards use 1/2/4/8 m panels fitted to the original protected run, with native yaw coefficients included for diagonal placement. A final overlapping panel retains coverage, while full-length rails and inset posts keep joins covered. Sloped stair rails retain their authored geometry. `audit-guards.ts` measures actual native-frame endpoint deviations and records their limits separately from route/body/visual acceptance. Small guard/balcony tubes use eight sides at the finest level and six at the next, preserving their radius and posts. Contiguous slide runs share bounded modules. AquaDome pairs adjacent same-color bands without removing any pane or beam. The Pearl uses continuous indexed inner and outer sector surfaces, capped only at sector and portal boundaries. Touching closed primitives remain independent mesh islands so internal caps cannot reverse adjacent visible faces during normal recalculation.

`tex/paint.py` authors the original marine enamel, wood, textile, tile, metal and wayfinding atlases. Reference pixels are never sampled. Fine deck surfaces repeat the finish within 4 m spans, including tapered fragments. Intentional semantic materials provide native flat colours; actual Principled base colours preserve fallback appearance. Far proxies also use representative per-kind colours, including pale/blue funnel bands, dark accommodation glazing, yellow lifeboats and six colored slide courses. The map-local `landmarks:cruise` asset uses the current `LANDMARK_TRIANGLES` rules, keeps materials through 80 m, and states the visible errors of its authored distant representations. Hidden furnishings do not inflate the visible error of a closed exterior. The shared texture budget remains 64 MiB of decoded RGBA mip data.

Rebuild from the repository root:

```sh
python3 assets/source/landmarks/cruise/author_layout.py
python3 assets/source/landmarks/cruise/tex/paint.py
bun assets/source/landmarks/cruise/audit-guards.ts
bun run art -- landmarks:cruise
bun run art -- landmarks:cruise --check
bun tools/cruise/packed-groups.ts
bun tools/cruise/scale.ts --features
bun test tests/unit/cruise-art.test.ts tests/unit/cruise-floor-facing.test.ts
```
