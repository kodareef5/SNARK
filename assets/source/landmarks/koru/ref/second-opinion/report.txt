Overall
The hand-overs are visible in pairs 4 and 12. The decisive tell is that the softer picture still has an entire bow rail and curled ornament that the sharper picture lacks. Pair 6 has a smaller change in wire strength, and pair 11 has a subtler one on the outer stay. For the other pairs I would not reliably identify a hand-over from the ship. A 50% letter is a forced choice. I excluded overall softness, moving clouds and the shifted skyline from the verdict.

The closest forestays, especially in pairs 1 and 9, look like broad ivory cord. The conspicuous feature is how evenly bright they stay over a long run of blue sky and green hillside. A sunlit wire can glint, and a grey wire can look dark on sky and light on land. The uniform bright stripe is less convincing. At smaller scales the clearest artificial feature is the regular string-of-beads appearance on the inner sky stay in 3B, 5A and 6A. I can follow a faint trace between those marks. I see neither a whole forestay switching off nor an open gap dividing one into separate pieces.
