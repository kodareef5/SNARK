Groups

A group's rank says how its members prove themselves. Open: from the moment they join. Staff, admin, map and cluster: once they sign in with their own password. Admins manage groups up to admin rank, map admins up to map rank, and cluster admins everything.

Add a member

Hours make a timed membership, as ArkApi's AddTimed does. A tribe joins open groups only. A staff membership holds on the map it was made on, unless a cluster admin makes it for every map.

Staff passwords

A member of a staff group claims their own password in game: they type claimpassword in the console, and the password is shown to them once. Nobody else ever sees it, here or anywhere. They use their group's powers after signing in with it: enablecheats and the password in game, or their name or ID and the password on these pages. Clearing a password ends their sign-ins, and they may claim a new one.
