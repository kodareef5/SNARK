Sound board

Every sound the game has, by who makes it and then by occasion, each with what sets it off in play (shared/sound/cues.ts). Beside each occasion, how this build hears it: its own takes, a stand-in of the owner's own (a placeholder, as a death cried with its pain, lower), a shared sound every owner without its own falls back to, or no take yet. Click one to hear it as the game would. The numbered buttons under it are its takes: the variants the game picks among, never the same twice running; hover one for what made it. Each owner links its brief, recipes and cue sheet, for whoever makes more.
