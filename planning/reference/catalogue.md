# Content catalogue

Generated by `bun run catalogue` from the class table in
`shared/content/classes.ts`. Do not edit it by hand: change the table and run
the tool.

Every kind of content has a class, as ARK's has. A definition's `type` is its
family and its class is a leaf of this table. The table says what a class must
declare, what it may, and which assets it expects. A field that is in neither
list is refused. Names and ids are this engine's own. The ARK column says what
the class corresponds to, so that what is known of ARK carries over.

There are 47 classes: 34 built, 6 partly built, and 7 stubs that
wait for a feature. The core pack holds 6086 definitions.

An asset slot is addressed `owner#slot`, or `owner#slot/key` for a slot with
several values: `core:raptor#clip/walk`. Levels of detail are inside the asset
and are never named by content.

A definition is named by its id. At the console and in a settings file it also
answers to an id it once had, to an alias (`aliases`, which any definition may
declare and no two may share), and to its id's own name where it alone answers
to that: `spear` for `core:spear`. Case is ignored. A creature and its variants
share a `tag`.

- **`item`** on shelf `items`. Anything that is carried.
- **`item/resource`** on shelf `items/resources`. A material: gathered, stacked, and spent in crafting. One a weapon fires, as a slingshot fires stone, has an ammo facet.
- **`item/consumable`** on shelf `items/consumables`. Something eaten, drunk or fed for what it does to a body.
- **`item/weapon`** on shelf `items/weapons`. Held in the hand to strike, shoot or throw. A hatchet or pick is a weapon that is also a tool. A thrown one with an ammo facet of its own flies and hits as an arrow does (the bola).

- **`item/ammo`** on shelf `items/ammo`. What a bow or gun fires.
