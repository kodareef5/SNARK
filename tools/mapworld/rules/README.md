# Shared ruin rules

Every map inherits `default.json`. Its `ruins/vocabulary.json` extends objects by key, including individual forms, assemblies and damage profiles. Lists with an `id` extend by id in their original order; new ids append. An override with `enabled: false` removes an inherited id. Other lists replace, so a city's district palette, an assembly's parts and a choice's allowed kinds remain explicit.

A fourth city supplies its categories, recipes, districts, budget, labels and places. It does not copy `forms`, `choices`, `assemblies`, `street`, `damage`, `interiors` or `growth`. Named compositions remain in each city's vocabulary. `event.travel` is an engine-space vector towards which the surge travels, not a compass bearing. Engine x points west and z north.

A form chooses shell openings, interior, front assembly, roof, roof marker, plan and optional terrace depth in floors. A choice matches label kind, recipe, size, height, neighbours, shoreline distance, trades, era, district or name. It selects a form and finish. The event field chooses the failure family by exposure band and shared spatial cell, with measured buildings upstream providing shelter. Height still comes from labels. This is an authored event, not a hydrodynamic reconstruction.

An assembly lists `[shape, u, y, v, finish, optional yaw]` in metres and degrees. `finish` uses the district finish. Dimensions and material properties live in `content/ruins.ts`. Add a large part there only if an existing part cannot express it economically. Roof markers and front assemblies use the same library as street remnants. `passage` lists local `[u,v]` waypoints through a street assembly; placement rejects obstacles along that passage, reserves it from later props and plants, and the room walk verifies it in the packed map.

`growth.zones` overrides the named resource zones. Candidates are frozen as listed resource nodes only after their actual listed collision form clears structures, movement paths and spawn points. `growth.canopy` supplies estimated visual radius and height, scaled with the node's form, to keep leaves out of tested routes as well. `growth.lowAroundPlace` selects low vegetation within a radius of a public place kind, such as a crane yard. Roof planters reuse the game's existing creeper and hardwood meshes as statics: the map format gives resource nodes no elevation. `sightlines` in the city vocabulary reserve views towards named compositions against props and tall growth. They do not demolish existing footprint buildings.

Assemblies with role `roof identity` stay together when route clearance removes a part. A clock or sign must not retain a floating cap after its support is cut away. This is an assembly rule, not a structural solver for the entire building.

To add a rule, add one choice with a stable id and narrow conditions, or extend one form or assembly. To add a place, add its footprint id to the map's `ruins/places.json` and its composition to that map's vocabulary. Build with `bun tools/mapworld/build.ts <map>`, then `bun tools/mapworld/verify.ts var/maps/<map>.snmap`. `proof-views.ts`, `views.ts` and `ruin-report.ts` produce the gallery and records. All selections and spatial decisions are seeded; no wall clock is used in the map bytes.
